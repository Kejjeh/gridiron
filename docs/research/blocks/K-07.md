# K-07: spread times total, and favoured in a low total (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen; the only runs

## Idea
Roadmap K-07, "Spread and total interplay", from the schedules, kept small.
The shipped K set has the team's implied total (`implied`) and its spread
(`spread`, + = favoured), so the total (2 × implied − spread) is already in
it linearly, and "favoured at an equal implied total" is the shipped
spread's own coefficient. What a linear model in those two cannot express is
a second-order shape: the spread mattering more or less as the total moves.
This block adds only that.

**What it can measure:** whether kicker points bend with the spread and the
total together, beyond the shipped linear terms.

**What it cannot measure:** the game's actual script, or the coach's
fourth-down choices (K-02's subject).

## Hypothesis
A kicker's share of his team's points is higher when touchdowns are scarce
(a low total) and his team controls the game (favoured): drives that stall
in range end in field goals, and a leading team kicks rather than goes for
it. So kicker points should rise with the spread more steeply in a low total
than in a high one. The line is the market's pre-game expectation of the
game, neither a player's volume nor his efficiency.

2019), known before the season.
