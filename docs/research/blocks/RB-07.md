# RB-07: spread, and spread and total times his passing-down target share (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen; the only runs
before this were `--coverage-only` (coverage and the leak check, no score).

## Idea
Roadmap RB-07, "Game-script volume from spread and total", from the
schedules. The roadmap's note: QUANT_FOUNDATIONS §1.4 expects about zero
(team volume regressed on the line has essentially no out-of-sample skill;
the line moves efficiency), so this is one cheap test. §1.5: before the
game the spread barely moves pass attempts and moves rush attempts by about
+0.25 per point of favouritism. The shipped RB set has the implied total
(`implied`), his overall target share (`tgt_share_l3`), carries, snaps and
expected points. It has no spread (the `+line` block, spread with wind and
temperature, failed 4 of 7 folds in FEATURE_EXPANSION.md) and nothing that
lets the line move a third-down back and an early-down back apart. RB-05
tested the passing-down target share alone and failed (4 of 7 folds).

**What it can measure:** whether the line's expected script, applied to a
back's passing-down role, adds to the shipped set.

**What it cannot measure:** the script that actually happens (the line is
an expectation), or snaps by situation (participation stops at 2025). The
passing-down target share stands in for the passing-down role; a back's
blocking on third down does not show in it.

## Hypothesis
At an equal implied total, a favoured team runs more and trails less, so its
early-down back gets more carries; an underdog throws more late, so its
passing-down back gets more targets. The spread's effect on a back should
therefore depend on his passing-down role, and a higher total (more plays,
more passes) should help the passing-down back more. All volume, fast
(rule #6): the share is season to date, the line is this week's.

## Features (`COLUMNS`)
| column | position | definition, as of the weeks before the row's week | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `team_spread` | RB | his team's closing spread, + = favoured (the schedule's `spread_line` for the home side, its negative for the away side) | nflverse pre-game schedule |
| `spread_x_pd_share` | RB | `team_spread` × his passing-down target share: his targets on 3rd or 4th down or in a half's last two minutes (`half_seconds_remaining` ≤ 120) ÷ his teams' such targets, over his games this season (RB-05's `pd_tgt_share_std`, `_p1a_pbp_roles.py`) | schedule, nflverse play-by-play, snap counts, box scores |
| `total_x_pd_share` | RB | (the closing total − the previous season's mean closing total) × the same share | same |

Definitions: a target and his games are `_p1a_pbp_roles.py`'s (a target is
play_type `pass`, `pass_attempt` 1, a receiver named, not a two-point try;
his games are those with offensive snaps or a carry, target or pass attempt).
The total is centred on the previous season's mean closing total (2018's for
2019), known before the season, so a product with the share does not also
carry the share's own level. The spread is centred by construction (the two
sides of a game sum to zero).

Missing when: no earlier game this season, or his teams had no passing-down
targets in those games (the two products). The spread is never missing for
these rows. Missing enters as 0 plus `<col>__known`.

Distributions seen before registering (no scores): `team_spread` mean 0.15,
SD 6.6, range −20.5 to +20.5; both products nonzero on 76% of rows.
