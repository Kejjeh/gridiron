# TE-03: red-zone and end-zone target share, tight ends (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen; the only runs
before this were `--coverage-only` (coverage and the leak check, no score).

## Idea
Roadmap TE-03, "Red-zone and end-zone target share", from play-by-play. It
uses the same definitions as WR-04 (`_p1a_pbp_roles.red_zone_shares`), but it
is its own block and its own test, on tight-end rows. The shipped TE set
already carries his overall target share (`tgt_share_l3`), air-yards share
(`ay_share_l3`) and expected points (`xfp_l3`, `xfp_season`, `xfp_last`),
which price every past target by its air yards and field position. RB-03
(the goal-line carry share) failed in 0 of 7 folds, read as expected points
already carrying that role. What this block adds is the role in the share
form, normalised for how often the team reaches the scoring area.

## Hypothesis
Tight ends' value leans on touchdowns, and the red-zone and end-zone roles
are sticky within a season. A tight end who draws a large share of his
team's scoring-area targets scores more than his overall share and his
expected points say. This is volume (a share of opportunities), so it updates
fast (rule #6): season to date and last 3.

## Features (`COLUMNS`)
| column | position | definition, as of the weeks before the row's week | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `rz_tgt_share_std` | TE | his targets with `yardline_100` ≤ 20 ÷ his teams' such targets, over his games this season | nflverse play-by-play, snap counts, box scores |
| `rz_tgt_share_l3` | TE | the same over his last 3 games | same |
| `ez_tgt_share_std` | TE | his end-zone targets (`air_yards` ≥ `yardline_100`: the throw reaches the goal line, from anywhere on the field) ÷ his teams' end-zone targets, over his games this season | same |
| `ez_tgt_share_l3` | TE | the same over his last 3 games | same |

Definitions (`_p1a_pbp_roles.py`): a target is play_type `pass`,
`pass_attempt` 1, a receiver named, not a two-point try. It matches the
box-score targets exactly. His games are the games he was on the field for on
offense: offensive snaps > 0 in the snap counts, or a box score with a carry,
target or pass attempt. This matters most here: 24% of the target tight
ends' games had snaps but no stat line, so a share over box-score games alone
would drop those games' team targets from his denominator.

Missing when: no earlier game this season (all four), or his teams had no
such targets in those games (the share). Missing enters as 0 plus
`<col>__known`.
