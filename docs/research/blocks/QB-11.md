# QB-11: forecast wind above 10 mph, and its product with his deep-pass share (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen; the only runs
before this were `--coverage-only` (coverage and the leak check, no score).

## Idea
Roadmap QB-11, "Wind from a forecast, deep passers only", from Open-Meteo.
The roadmap's note: weather failed in general (the `+line` block, the spread
with the schedule's observed wind and temperature, 4 of 7 folds in
FEATURE_EXPANSION.md); this is one interaction. The shipped QB set has the
team's implied total (the market lowers totals for wind), expected points
(which price every throw's air yards) and nothing on the weather or on how
deep he throws.

**What it can measure:** whether wind at kickoff costs a quarterback who
throws deep more than one who throws short, beyond the implied total.

**What it cannot measure:** the forecast a lineup decision sees days before
kickoff. Open-Meteo's archived forecast is assembled from the first hours
of each model run, so it is close to the observation (a better stand-in
than none, not a Wednesday forecast). Nor gusts, the wind's direction to the
field, or the wind inside a stadium bowl (the model's 10 m wind is for its
grid cell).

## Hypothesis
Wind above about 10 mph shortens and scatters long passes most, so a
quarterback whose aimed passes often travel 20+ yards in the air should lose
more in wind than one who throws short. The wind is a pre-game fact of this
week's game. The deep share is how he throws (a share of his attempts, a
volume split, not an efficiency), season to date and shrunk toward the
league's rate (rule #6).

## Features (`COLUMNS`)
| column | position | definition, as of the weeks before the row's week | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `wind10` | QB | the venue's wind at kickoff: the mean of the hourly 10 m wind speed at the kickoff hour and the two hours after it (mph), minus 10, floored at 0; 0 indoors (roof dome or closed) | Open-Meteo historical forecast (`gfs_seamless`), ERA5 reanalysis where the forecast has no values; nflverse pre-game schedule (kickoff, roof, stadium); `data/reference/stadiums.csv` |
| `wind10_x_deep` | QB | `wind10` × (his deep share − the league's rate): his deep share is (his aimed passes with `air_yards` ≥ 20 + 50 × the league's rate) ÷ (his aimed passes + 50), over his games this season; the league's rate is the share of all aimed passes of the previous season with 20+ air yards | the above; nflverse play-by-play, box scores |

Definitions (`_p5_schedule_weather.py`): an aimed pass is play_type `pass`,
`pass_attempt` 1, not a sack, not a spike, not a two-point try, with air
yards recorded. The kickoff hour is the schedule's `gameday` and `gametime`
(Eastern) in UTC, floored to the hour. The venue is the schedule's
`stadium_id` (coordinates: Wikidata, revision on every row), except a
neutral-site game listed at the home team's own stadium (the six 2025
international games from week 4 on), whose venue and weather are unknown.
The 10 mph floor and the 20-yard line are fixed before any score; wind below
about 10 mph is commonly taken not to move passing.

Weather data by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0). Weeks 4–18 of 2019–2025 (1,535
games): 1,045 from the archived forecast, 5 from the observed reanalysis
(the five 2019 international games, before the forecast archive covers
Europe and Mexico), 479 indoors (0 and 0), and 6 unknown (the 2025
international games listed at a home stadium); no outdoor game is left
without a value. Over the 1,050 outdoor games the forecast wind is above 10
mph in 22.6% (above 15 mph in 5.1%) and the precipitation 1 mm or more in
6.4%. Checked against the schedule's observed wind (955 outdoor games where
it has one): correlation 0.73, means 7.4 and 8.6 mph.

Missing when: the venue is unknown (`wind10` and the product), or he has no
earlier aimed pass this season while `wind10` > 0 (the product; 0 when
`wind10` is 0). Missing enters as 0 plus `<col>__known`.

Distributions seen before registering (no scores): `wind10` is nonzero on
15.9% of QB rows (mean 0.54, 95th percentile 4.3, maximum 20.2);
`wind10_x_deep` on 15.4% (SD 0.043, range −0.52 to 0.63). His deep share:
mean 0.116, SD 0.024; the league's rate is 0.113–0.123 across 2018–2024.
