# RB-02: return-from-injury ramp and the fill-in's give-back, running backs (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen; the only runs
before this were `--coverage-only` (coverage and the leak check, no score)
and counts of how often each column is nonzero (no actual points read).

## Idea
Roadmap RB-02, "Return-from-injury ramp, and the backup's retained share"
(kind A, workstream C), cast as features because the adjustment is naturally
one: a flag on the returning back and a give-back on his fill-in. The
shipped RB set reads a returning back's last three games and last game
(`xfp_l3`, `snap_l3`, `carries_l3`, `xfp_last`, `opp_last`, `snap_last`):
for him those are the games before the absence, the last one often the game
he was hurt in, and nothing in the set says he was away. For his fill-in the
same columns hold the weeks he started, and `vacated_pickup` (teammates Out
or Doubtful this week) drops to zero the week the starter is back. The
returner study (`RETURNER_BACKTEST.md`) covered players with no game yet
this season; this block covers absences inside a season. `INJURY_EFFECTS.md`
(2023-25, descriptive, small n) saw running backs' next six games after an
ankle or knee absence run at 0.77-0.79 of their earlier points per game.

## Hypothesis
A back's first game back from an injury absence carries less volume than
his pre-absence averages say (snaps and touches ramp up), more after a
longer absence and after a muscle strain; and the fill-in who took his work
gives most of it back that week, which the fill-in's recent averages cannot
know. Both are volume (rule #6), read from this week's report and roster.

## Features (`COLUMNS`)
| column | position(s) | definition, as of the weeks before the row's week | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ret_back` | RB | 1 when this is his first game back from an injury absence: he played earlier this season, his team has played at least one game since his last one (byes do not count), and between his last game and this week's report he was listed with an injury on a final report (any designation; rest days, personal matters, illness, COVID protocols and coach's decisions do not count) or was on an injury list (RES, PUP, NFI) on a weekly roster. 0 otherwise: healthy scratches, suspensions and season debuts are not returns here | box scores and snap counts ("played" = a box-score line or any snap), injury reports, weekly rosters, schedule |
| `ret_missed` | RB | the team's games he missed in that absence, capped at 8; 0 when `ret_back` is 0 | same |
| `ret_soft` | RB | `ret_back`, and the injury on his latest report entry since his last game (through this week's report) is a muscle strain: hamstring, groin, calf, quadriceps, thigh, pectoral, oblique, abdomen, adductor, hip flexor (whole words of the report text); else 0 | injury reports |
| `mate_back_opp` | RB | his share of his team's running-back carries + targets in its last game × the carries + targets per game this season of the running backs expected back this week from injury. Expected back: a box score for the team earlier this season, no part in its last game, injured that week (as for `ret_back`), on its 53 this week (status ACT or INA, INA read as ACT) and not Out or Doubtful on this week's final report | box scores, snap counts, injury reports, weekly rosters |

Missing when: never. Every RB row gets all four columns, so none gets a
`__known` indicator.

Shared code: `scripts/research/blocks/_p2b_events_ramps_defense.py`
(`ramp`, with `played`, `hurt`, `availability`). WR-13 and TE-12 use the same
definitions on their own positions. Snap counts map PFR ids to gsis ids
through the crosswalk, then nflverse's player table. The helper loads injury
reports one season at a time: `Context.injuries` over 2019-2025 returns the
2025 rows alone (a runner loader bug, reported, not changed here).

On the 8,412 RB rows of 2019-2025: `ret_back` is 1 on 352 rows (2019 35,
2020 69, 2021 72, 2022 47, 2023 46, 2024 44, 2025 39); of those, 140 missed

`ret_soft` is 1 on 92 rows (6-19 a season). `mate_back_opp` is nonzero on
824 rows (65-156 a season), at most 25.0.

### Correction 2026-11-02: one bug-fix re-run

The near-miss bug hunt (after round 1, before any round-2 score) found a bug in the shared helper `_p2b_events_ramps_defense.hurt`, which says who was injured. Every RES row on a weekly roster counted as an injury list. From 2020 the rosters file the COVID-19 list and suspensions as RES with a description code (R59; R30, R40), so COVID absences and suspensions counted as injuries, against this pre-registration (illness and COVID protocols do not count; suspensions are not returns). Fixed in `fb26c4b`: only injury lists count (injured reserve, with or without a return designation, PUP, non-football injury), by code from 2021 and by status before; the 2019-2020 non-football-injury status (RSN) now counts as its later code does. This block's module and the pre-registration above are unchanged.

- `ret_back`: 24 of 8,412 rows (2020 11, 2021 13)

- `ret_soft`: 0 of 8,412 rows
