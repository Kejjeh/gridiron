# QB-03: play-caller's neutral pass rate over expected, career and this season (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen; the only runs
before this were `--coverage-only` (coverage and the leak check, no score).

## Idea
Roadmap QB-03, "Pass rate over expected by play-caller", from play-by-play
`pass_oe` and the play-caller table (`data/reference/play_callers.csv`,
workstream B). The `+team` block (FEATURE_EXPANSION.md) gave the model the
offense's pass rate over its last 3 games and failed (4 of 7 folds). This
block measures the PERSON who calls the plays, so the tendency follows a
coordinator who changes teams and carries his history into a season. The
shipped QB set carries the quarterback's own volume this season (expected
points over the last 3 games, the season and the last game; carries; snaps)
and the implied team total; it has no prior from before this season and
nothing about who calls the plays.

## Hypothesis
How often a play-caller throws when the situation does not force it is a
habit of the person, stable across seasons and teams. A pass-leaning
play-caller gives his quarterback more attempts than three games of this
season's volume and the line say, and his career is a better estimate of
the habit early in a season or after he changes teams. This is volume (a
play-calling choice), not efficiency (rule #6).

## Features (`COLUMNS`)
| column | position | definition, as of the weeks before the row's week | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `pc_proe_career` | QB | the week's play-caller (OPC) for the QB's team: his neutral `pass_oe` minus the league's neutral `pass_oe` in the same season, summed over his plays in every season before this one in which the table names him play-caller (any team), ÷ (his plays + 100) | nflverse play-by-play 2019–2025, the play-caller table |
| `pc_proe_season` | QB | the same over his plays this season with this team in weeks before the row's week, against the league's neutral `pass_oe` over this season's weeks before the row's week, ÷ (his plays + 100) | same |

Neutral play (`_tendencies.py`): `play_type` pass or run (no-plays, spikes
and kneels never count), not a two-point try, not an aborted snap,
`pass_oe` present, 0.2 ≤ wp ≤ 0.8, quarters 1–3, not the last two minutes
of the first half. `pass_oe` is nflverse's pass rate over expected (pass ×
100 − `xpass` × 100). The 100 plays of shrinkage pull a small sample toward
the league (0); they were set before any score from the per-play noise
(about 49 points) and a plausible spread between play-callers (about 5).

Who is in charge: the person the table names as OPC for (season, team,
week); a team-week without an OPC row is unknown (never the OC, never the
head coach). The table names a play-caller for 3,001 of the 3,070
team-games of weeks 4–18, 2019–2025 (none for 2021 MIA, 2022 JAX, 2023 NYG
and a few partial seasons). 90 people called plays in 2019–2025, 32 of them
for more than one team. A team-season has 382–606 neutral plays (mean 498).

Missing when: the team-week has no named play-caller; for the career
column, also when he has no earlier season as play-caller in the table,
which is every 2019 row (the table starts in 2019, and 2018 play-by-play
names no play-caller); for the season column, also when he has not called
a game for this team yet this season (his first week in charge). Missing
enters as 0 plus `<col>__known`.
