# QB-07: hook risk, from in-game reliefs and a lost QB1 spot this season (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen; the only runs
before this were `--coverage-only` (coverage and the leak check, no score).

## Idea
Roadmap QB-07, "Benching or hook risk" (depth charts, injuries), kind A: a
fitted adjustment. The adjustment is naturally a feature, his own record this
season of not finishing starts and of losing the job, so it is tested as a
feature block. The roadmap's note ("who starts" markets, if a free tier has
them) does not apply to 2019-2025: the free odds tiers have no history
(DATA_INVENTORY.md). The free signals are play-by-play (who threw) and the
pre-game depth charts.

The shipped QB set already carries this week's depth-chart rank
(`depth_rank`: a benched starter shows 2, so this block does not read this
week's chart), his snap share over his last 3 games and in his last game
(`snap_l3`, `snap_last`: a start he did not finish shows there for three
games) and his points over expected (`fpoe_season`, the poor play that leads
to a hook).

## Hypothesis
A starter who has already been pulled or benched this season is more likely
to be pulled again, and a quarterback who does not finish games scores less
than his per-game volume says. Season to date, a longer memory than the
shipped three-game snap share. Volume, not efficiency (rule #6).

## Features (`COLUMNS`)
| column | position(s) | definition, as of the weeks before the row's week | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `qb_start_db_share` | QB | his dropbacks ÷ his team's dropbacks in the games he started this season (a start: he had his team's first dropback of the game) | nflverse play-by-play |
| `qb_relieved_std` | QB | his starts this season in which another passer of his team had 5 or more dropbacks, that passer's first coming while the team was not ahead by 17 or more (`score_differential`): resting a starter in a blowout is not a hook | nflverse play-by-play |
| `qb_benched_std` | QB | 1 if, in an earlier week this season, he was healthy (no Out, Doubtful or Questionable on that week's final injury report; no reserve or departure status on that week's roster) and on a team's pre-game depth chart but not its QB1, after being QB1 on a chart in a still earlier week; else 0 | depth charts, injury reports, weekly rosters |

Dropbacks: `qb_dropback` 1, not a two-point try, credited to `passer_id` (the
dropback player, scrambles included). nflverse's schedule starting-QB ids are
not used. They agree with the first dropback in 98.5-100% of team-games in
2019-2023 and 2025, but in 93.2% in 2024: one team's backup is named for five
games its starter played throughout. The 2025 depth charts are the latest
snapshot labelled for each week, as the runner labels them (published
before the week's first game day).

What is not separated: an injury exit counts as a relief (a hook in the
fantasy sense: he did not finish). The chart flag carries the charts' noise:
a team that lists the other quarterback first in a week its starter was hurt
but not designated reads as a lost spot. Checked by hand on 2024: the flagged
quarterbacks include the season's benchings, and one starter flagged after a
week the chart listed a hurt veteran first.

Feature values only (no outcomes): `qb_relieved_std` is above 0 on 17-30% of
QB rows per season (at most 2 to 4); `qb_benched_std` is 1 on 5-13%;
`qb_start_db_share` has a median of 0.99 where known.

Missing when: `qb_start_db_share` when he has no start this season before the
week (8-16% of QB rows: backups the universe includes); the other two are 0
then. Missing enters as 0 plus `<col>__known`.

Runner bugs, worked around: `ctx.depth_charts` over several seasons that
include 2025 drops every 2025 row (the timestamped layout has no
`game_type`), and `ctx.injuries` over several seasons that include 2025
drops 2019-2024. The helper reads both one season at a time.

Shared code: `scripts/research/blocks/_p2a_events_offense.py` (`passer_games`,
`starters`, `chart_qbs`, `reports`, `roster_away`).
