# K-12: artificial turf, and an outdoor game in December or January (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen; the only runs
before this were `--coverage-only` (coverage and the leak check, no score).

## Idea
Roadmap K-12, "Surface and late-season outdoor splits", from the schedules
(surface, roof). The shipped K set has the roof (`dome`: 1 for a dome or a
closed roof) and nothing on the surface or the calendar. Most domes have
artificial turf, so `turf` overlaps `dome`; the block adds what the roof
does not say: the outdoor turf fields, and the cold, late-season outdoor
games.

**What it can measure:** whether kicker points differ on artificial turf,
and in outdoor games in December and January, beyond the roof.

**What it cannot measure:** temperature, wind or the state of the field
(K-05 has wind and precipitation); the late-season flag is a calendar stand-in
for cold, the same in Miami as in Green Bay.

## Hypothesis
Kicking off artificial turf gives a steadier plant than grass, and outdoor
games in December and January are colder (the ball carries less), windier
and on worn grass, so field-goal range shrinks and makes fall. Kicker points
should be a little higher on turf and lower in outdoor late-season games.
These are pre-game facts of the venue and the date.
