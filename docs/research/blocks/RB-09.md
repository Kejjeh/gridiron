# RB-09: own line's run blocking, the team's yards before contact per RB carry (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen; the only runs
before this were `--coverage-only` (coverage and the leak check, no score).

## Idea
Roadmap RB-09, "Own line's run blocking", from PFR yards before contact
(team sums). The roadmap's other half, ESPN's run-block win rates, is not a
free source: deferred, as the roadmap says. The shipped RB set carries the
back's own points over expected (`fpoe_season`), Next Gen Stats rush yards
over expected (`ngs_ryoe`, only for backs above NGS's volume threshold), the
implied team total and his volume. FEATURE_EXPANSION's `+pfr` tested the
back's OWN yards before contact (his season mean) and failed (1 of 7 folds).
This is the team's figure: every back's carries behind the same line.

## Hypothesis
Yards before contact are mostly the line's and the scheme's. A team whose
backs gain more before contact gives its back more yards per carry than his
own short, noisy record says, so the team figure (a bigger sample than one
back's carries) and last season's team figure add to the projection. This
is efficiency, so it enters only as a slow prior (rule #6): season to date
and the prior season, relative to the league.

## Features (`COLUMNS`)
| column | position(s) | definition, as of the weeks before the row's week | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `team_ybc_std` | RB | his team's running backs' (box-score position RB or FB in that game) rushing yards before contact per carry in the team's games this season, minus the league's figure over the same weeks | PFR advanced rushing, weekly (`rushing_yards_before_contact`, `carries`), team sums; crosswalk pfr -> gsis (rule #3); player box scores |
| `team_ybc_prior` | RB | the same over his team's whole previous regular season, minus that season's league figure (2018 for the 2019 rows) | same |

"His team" is the row's team (the team he plays for that week); the prior
season is that franchise's, not his own previous team's.

Missing when: never on the scored rows (every team has played by week 4,
and PFR covers 2018 on). Were a value missing it would enter as 0 plus
`<col>__known`.

Shared code: `scripts/research/blocks/_p1b_ftn_pfr_opponent.py`
(`back_rushes`, `league_as_of`). The runner's Context has no PFR loader;
`pfr_rush` loads PFR's weekly rushing through the Context's frame store
under the post-game `weekly` classification, so the leak check cuts it like
every other post-game frame (checked: a variant that reads the row's own
week is flagged). The helper maps team codes itself (PFR writes OAK in
2018-2019; the runner's `normalize_teams` does not rewrite pandas 3 string
columns), so the Raiders' 2019 rows and their 2020 prior are joined.
Running backs carry 10,805-11,873 times a season in 2018-2025 (98.9% of
PFR's carries map to a gsis id in 2018, 99.9% or more after); the league
figure moves from 2.07 (2019) to 2.58 (2022) yards per carry.
