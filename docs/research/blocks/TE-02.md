# TE-02: routes vs blocking, route participation (assessed 2026-11-02)

**Status: NOT TESTABLE with free data.** No block module, no run. This file
records why, so the idea is not retried as a forced proxy.

## Idea
Roadmap TE-02, "Routes vs blocking (route participation)", from
participation (to 2025), prior only. The measure it needs is his routes run
per pass snap (or per team dropback) last season. A tight end who runs a
route on most dropbacks is a receiver; one who stays in to block is not,
even at the same snap share.

## Why it cannot be measured
Checked on the research cache: nflverse participation joined to
regular-season play-by-play.
- **`offense_players`** (100% of dropbacks) says who was on the field. It
  does not say whether a player ran a route or stayed in to block.
- **`route`** is the targeted receiver's route only: in 2024 it is filled on
  98.7% of targeted dropbacks and on 12.4% of the others, and it never
  names more than one route (0 of 2024's 19,110 filled rows). Over all
  participation rows it is 36–43% filled (2022–2025). It describes one
  player per play, never the rest.
- **FTN charting** (the free nflverse subset) has per-play flags only: play
  action, screens, the number of pass rushers and blitzers, and so on.
  Nothing is per player. NGS weekly receiving (cushion, separation, air
  yards, YAC) and PFR advanced receiving (broken tackles, drops) publish no
  routes and no pass-blocking snaps.

So routes run per pass snap cannot be computed:
- With on-field dropbacks as the "routes" (the only free proxy), routes per
  pass snap is 1 for everyone.
- With team dropbacks as the denominator, it becomes his share of team
  dropbacks on the field. That is the in-season `route_share_l3` that
  FEATURE_EXPANSION's `+routes` block already tested, and it failed.
- For a tight end, that proxy counts every pass-blocking snap as a route:
  the opposite of what the idea separates.
- From the targeted plays alone, P(targeted | on field) = P(route) ×
  P(targeted | route). The two factors cannot be separated.

## What would unlock it
Per-player route-or-block data:
- PFF or the FTN Data API (paid, deferred by the roadmap);
- or a per-player route flag in a future nflverse participation release.

A different free idea, his share of team pass plays on the field against his
share of team runs on the field (deployment by play type), would be a new,
separately declared block. It is not this one, and it was not built or run.
