# QB-02: designed runs and scrambles per game, season to date (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen; the only runs
before this were `--coverage-only` (coverage and the leak check, no score).

## Idea
Roadmap QB-02, "Designed runs vs scrambles", from play-by-play
`qb_scramble`. The shipped QB set prices rushing through `carries_l3` (his
box-score carries over his last 3 games) and expected points (`xfp_l3`,
`xfp_season`, `xfp_last`). Box-score carries mix three different things: the
designed runs a coordinator calls, the scrambles a quarterback improvises when
a dropback breaks down, and kneels. In 2024, kneels were 405 of 2,352
quarterback box-score carries (17%), and they never score. The roadmap's FTN
half (`is_qb_out_of_pocket`) is not used: it starts in 2022, and
play-by-play's `qb_scramble` covers 2019–2025.

## Hypothesis
Designed runs are a play call: scheme, sticky within a season. Scrambles
react to coverage and pressure and are noisier. With kneels out and the two
kinds apart, the ridge can weight the repeatable part of a quarterback's
rushing volume, which `carries_l3` blurs. Both are volume, so they update
fast (rule #6). Season to date rather than last 3, because quarterback rush
counts per game are small.

## Features (`COLUMNS`)
| column | position | definition, as of the weeks before the row's week | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `qb_designed_pg` | QB | his designed runs (play_type `run`, `rush_attempt` 1, not `qb_scramble`, not a two-point try, not an aborted snap; kneels are play_type `qb_kneel` and never count) ÷ his games this season | nflverse play-by-play, snap counts, box scores |
| `qb_scramble_pg` | QB | his scrambles (play_type `run`, `qb_scramble` 1, not a two-point try) ÷ his games this season | same |

His games are the games he was on the field for on offense: offensive snaps
> 0 in the snap counts (pfr id → gsis id through the one crosswalk), or a box
score with a carry, target or pass attempt (`_p1a_pbp_roles.games_played`).
Checked on 2024 before registering: designed + scrambles + aborted snaps +
kneels equals his box-score carries in every quarterback-week (664 of 664).

Missing when: no earlier game this season. Missing enters as 0 plus
`<col>__known`.
