# TE-07: target bump when a wide receiver is out (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen; the only runs
before this were `--coverage-only` (coverage and the leak check, no score).

## Idea
Roadmap TE-07, "Target bump when a WR is out" (workstream C; "Shares
WR-03"): the vacated wide-receiver target share this week times the tight
end's share of the non-WR targets, season to date. The shipped TE set
carries his target share and air-yards share (last 3 games), expected points
and `vacated_pickup`, which pools every Out/Doubtful teammate's carries plus
targets (last 3 games) and hands them to all active teammates by their share
of the team's opportunities. It does not say whether the missing work was a
receiver's, or that the tight end is the one the team turns to. TE-03 (his
red-zone and end-zone target shares) failed (better on both metrics in 0 of
7 folds).

## Hypothesis
When a wide receiver is out, part of his targets goes to the players who are
not wide receivers, and within that group mostly to the tight end who
already gets most of it. The tight end gains in proportion to the vacated
receiver share and to his share of the team's non-WR targets, most in the
absence's first week, before his own last game shows the change. Volume,
season to date (rule #6).

## Features (`COLUMNS`)
| column | position(s) | definition, as of the weeks before the row's week | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `te_vac_wr_new` | TE | V_new × his share of the team's non-WR targets | nflverse play-by-play, snap counts, box scores, final injury reports, weekly rosters |
| `te_vac_wr_all` | TE | V_all × his share of the team's non-WR targets | same |

V_new: the sum, over his team's wide receivers unavailable this week who
played the team's previous game, of each one's target share this season (his
targets ÷ his teams' targets, over his games for this team). V_all: the same
over every unavailable wide receiver, however long he has been out. His share
of the non-WR targets: his targets ÷ (his teams' targets − their targets to
players whose box-score position that game was WR), over his games for the
team this season. A teammate is a wide receiver by the box-score position of
his latest game for the team: the tag only names the group, and how much he
matters is his target share (rule #4).

Unavailable (`_p2a_events_offense.py`): Out or Doubtful on the week's final
injury report; or, on the week's weekly roster, a reserve status (RES, EXE,
and the 2019-2020 codes PUP, SUS, NWT, RSN, RSR, E01, E14, RET) or a
departure status (CUT, TRD, TRC, UFA, RFA), or listed on another team only.
Never INA, never DEV, and Questionable is not Out (rule #11).

Feature values only (no outcomes): `te_vac_wr_new` is above 0 on 16-27% of TE
rows per season (median 0.012-0.021 where above 0, at most 0.30);
`te_vac_wr_all` on 51-70%. Checked by hand on 2023: the largest values are
the tight ends in the first week their team's top wide receiver was out.

Missing when: a receiver is vacated but his own non-WR share is undefined
(no game for the team yet, or no non-WR targets in his games): at most 0.5%
of TE rows in a season. Missing enters as 0 plus `<col>__known`.

Runner bug, worked around: `ctx.injuries` over several seasons that include
2025 returns 2025 only; the helper reads the reports one season at a time.
