# TE-12: return ramp and the fill-in's give-back, tight ends (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen; the only runs
before this were `--coverage-only` (coverage and the leak check, no score)
and counts of how often each column is nonzero (no actual points read).

## Idea
Roadmap TE-12, "Return ramps" (kind A, workstream C), with RB-02's
definitions and its own block ID, cast as features. The shipped TE set reads
a returning tight end's last three games and last game (`xfp_l3`,
`snap_l3`, `tgt_share_l3`, `xfp_last`, `opp_last`, `snap_last`): the games
before the absence, with nothing saying he was away. A team's tight ends
split one role, so the one who filled in often loses it outright when the
starter is back, and `vacated_pickup` drops to zero that week.
`INJURY_EFFECTS.md` had too few tight-end returns to measure.

## Hypothesis
A tight end's first game back from an injury absence carries fewer snaps
and targets than his pre-absence averages say (more after a longer absence
or a muscle strain), and the tight end who filled in gives the work back the
same week. Both are volume (rule #6), read from this week's report and
roster.

## Features (`COLUMNS`)
| column | position(s) | definition, as of the weeks before the row's week | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ret_back` | TE | 1 when this is his first game back from an injury absence: he played earlier this season, his team has played at least one game since his last one (byes do not count), and between his last game and this week's report he was listed with an injury on a final report (any designation; rest days, personal matters, illness, COVID protocols and coach's decisions do not count) or was on an injury list (RES, PUP, NFI) on a weekly roster. 0 otherwise: healthy scratches, suspensions and season debuts are not returns here | box scores and snap counts ("played" = a box-score line or any snap), injury reports, weekly rosters, schedule |
| `ret_missed` | TE | the team's games he missed in that absence, capped at 8; 0 when `ret_back` is 0 | same |
| `ret_soft` | TE | `ret_back`, and the injury on his latest report entry since his last game (through this week's report) is a muscle strain: hamstring, groin, calf, quadriceps, thigh, pectoral, oblique, abdomen, adductor, hip flexor (whole words of the report text); else 0 | injury reports |
| `mate_back_opp` | TE | his share of his team's tight-end carries + targets in its last game × the carries + targets per game this season of the tight ends expected back this week from injury. Expected back: a box score for the team earlier this season, no part in its last game, injured that week (as for `ret_back`), on its 53 this week (status ACT or INA, INA read as ACT) and not Out or Doubtful on this week's final report | box scores, snap counts, injury reports, weekly rosters |

Missing when: never. Every TE row gets all four columns, so none gets a
`__known` indicator.

Shared code: `scripts/research/blocks/_p2b_events_ramps_defense.py`
(`ramp`), the definitions RB-02 and WR-13 use on their positions. The helper
loads injury reports one season at a time: `Context.injuries` over
2019-2025 returns the 2025 rows alone (a runner loader bug, reported, not
changed here).

On the 6,648 TE rows of 2019-2025: `ret_back` is 1 on 242 rows (2019 24,
2020 34, 2021 62, 2022 35, 2023 23, 2024 31, 2025 33); of those, 127 missed
one game, 43 two, 27 three, 13 four and 32 five or more (9 at the cap).
`ret_soft` is 1 on 45 rows (4-9 a season). `mate_back_opp` is nonzero on 419
rows (29-78 a season), at most 10.4. The test has little power: about 35
returns a season.
