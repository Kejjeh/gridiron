# TE-05: tight-end-friendly play-caller history (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen; the only runs
before this were `--coverage-only` (coverage and the leak check, no score).

## Idea
Roadmap TE-05, "Tight-end-friendly play-caller history", workstream B: the
share of his teams' targets that went to tight ends before this season,
following the play-caller across teams. The shipped TE set carries the
tight end's own target and air-yards share over the last 3 games, expected
points and depth-chart rank this season; nothing about the person's habit,
and no history from before this season. A linear model cannot combine the
play-caller's tight-end share with this tight end's place among his team's
tight ends, so the block gives the product.

## Hypothesis
Some play-callers build their passing game around tight ends and keep doing
so on a new team. The tight end who leads his team's tight-end targets
under such a play-caller should hold or grow his volume; under a
play-caller who looks elsewhere his share is less safe. Volume (rule #6),
as a slow prior (prior seasons only).

## Features (`COLUMNS`)
| column | position | definition, as of the weeks before the row's week | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `pc_te_tgt_share` | TE | the week's play-caller (OPC) for the tight end's team: over the targets of his offenses in every season before this one in which the table names him play-caller (any team), the share that went to tight ends minus the league's share in the same season: summed deviations ÷ (his targets + 100) | nflverse play-by-play and box scores 2019–2024, the play-caller table |
| `te_implied_tgt_share` | TE | (the league's tight-end share of targets over the season before + `pc_te_tgt_share`) × his share of his teams' tight-end targets over the games he played this season | the above, nflverse play-by-play, snap counts, box scores |

Target (`_tendencies.py`): a play-by-play pass attempt (`play_type` pass)
with a receiver named, not a two-point try. A tight-end target is one whose
receiver's position in that week's box score is TE (the label defines the
group, the same way the TE rows are TE rows; no role is read from it).
Every target of 2019–2025 has a box score (120,921 of 120,921). The league's
tight-end share was 0.210–0.239 a season. His games are the games he was
on the field for on offense (offensive snaps > 0 in the snap counts through
the one crosswalk, or a box score with a carry, target or pass attempt).
The 100 targets of shrinkage were set before any score.

Who is in charge: as in QB-03 (the table's OPC; no row means unknown).

Missing when: the team-week has no named play-caller, or he has no earlier
season as play-caller in the table, which is every 2019 row (the table
starts in 2019); `te_implied_tgt_share` also when the tight end has no
earlier game this season or his teams threw no tight-end target in those
games. Missing enters as 0 plus `<col>__known`.
