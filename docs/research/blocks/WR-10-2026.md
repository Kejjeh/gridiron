# WR-10-2026: WR-10's frozen fit on 2026 weeks 1–3 (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen. The only runs
before this were `--holdout-2026 --coverage-only` (2026 coverage, feature
counts, the leak check and the two reproduction checks, no score) and a dry
run of the scoring code on random synthetic outcomes (no real points).

## Idea
WR-10 passed its historical bar (`results/WR-10.json`: 7 of 7 folds; see
`WR-10.md`). Its Result says the frozen fit is re-tested unchanged on 2026
weeks 1–3 before it can get a DECISIONS.md row. Those weeks were played from
9 to 28 September 2026, and all 48 results are final. WR-10 was designed and
scored on 2019–2025 only, and no run of the harness has scored a 2026 row.
This is ground rule 3's untouched-season check for an early-season prior,
which cannot run record-only in a live week 1–3 before 2027.

The harness is `scripts/research/early_season_backtest.py --holdout-2026`;
its docstring's "2026 HOLDOUT" section holds the method.

## Hypothesis
The fit that won every historical fold also wins on 2026. Draft capital
gives a rookie a week-1 number, and last season, discounted, gives a
veteran one.

## Test
- Rows: every 2026 week 1–3 WR player-week with a box score and a Sleeper
  projection, built exactly as WR-10's rows were: 456 rows, 222 of them with
  Sleeper 5+.
- Columns: WR-10's (`page`, `ppg_prev`, `rookie_capital`, `rookie_forty`),
  with its definitions and sources, for 2026. `page` comes from the 2026 box
  scores before the week, recomputed by today's `gridiron.projection` as the
  historical test did (not read from the archived boards). `ppg_prev` comes
  from 2025, and the rookie columns from the 2026 draft and combine. Missing
  enters as 0 plus `__known`, as in the training seasons.
- Systems, per week, all floored at 0. Nothing is refit except `today_fit`:
  - `cand`: WR-10's frozen fit (`frozen_fit.cand` in `results/WR-10.json`,
    fitted on all of 2019–2025), applied unchanged.
  - `prior`: the plain prior's frozen fit (`frozen_fit.prior`), unchanged.
  - `today`: the page as served, or the stored null (`frozen_fit.null`, the
    2019–2025 mean) where it abstains.
  - `today_fit`: the page refit. The JSON does not store this fit, so
    [page, page__known] is fitted on all of 2019–2025, the way the frozen fit
    was.

  Sleeper's projection is context only, never part of the bar.
- Metrics, pooled over the 2026 rows: start/sit pairwise within a week
  among Sleeper 5+ (a tie is a miss), MAE and bias. They are reported over
  weeks 1–3, over weeks 2–3 and for each week.
- Bar (both must hold):
  1. `cand` beats both `today` and `today_fit` on MAE and on pairwise, over
     weeks 1–3 and again over weeks 2–3.
  2. `cand` beats `prior` on MAE and on pairwise over weeks 1–3.

  These are WR-10's conditions 1 and 2, applied to the one held-out season.
  Its condition 3 (bias by band) is reported but not judged, because one
  season's rookie bands have 5 to 29 rows. Whether the plain prior beats
  the page is reported too.
- Refusals: a scored run refuses unless this doc, the harness and
  `results/WR-10.json` are committed and unchanged. The 2019–2025 rows
  rebuilt today must have WR-10's digest (`702508506f20e8a1`), and a refit
  on them must reproduce the stored fit. The leak check must pass for 2026
  weeks 1, 2 and 3.
- Coverage seen before registering (no scores):
  - rows (weeks 1, 2, 3): 151/152/153; 222 with Sleeper 5+
  - `page`: 59% (by week: 0%, 87%, 90%)
  - `ppg_prev`: 81%
  - `rookie_capital` (the rookie share): 14%; `rookie_forty`: 7%
  - band sizes (rows): last season's ppg none 85, <5 160, 5–10 138, 10–15
    60, 15–20 13; rookies R1 10, R2 5, R3–4 29, R5–7 13, undrafted 7;
    veterans 392
  - leak check: passed (2026 weeks 1, 2 and 3); data digest
    `14305206958017e9`. The training digest is WR-10's, and the refit
    reproduces the stored fit.
  - Sleeper's 2026 week-1 projections for QB, RB, WR and TE were not in the
    research cache. The coverage run fetched them on 2026-11-02 from the
    public endpoint every other week came from (also after the games).

## Whatever the outcome
Nothing changes on the page now: these priors are for 2027. If WR-10 passes
here, it stays a contender for the 2027 week 1–3 board. If it fails, it is
not used as it stands, and a redesign would be a new pre-registration.
Either way, its DECISIONS.md row comes in the offseason (phase 4).

## Result

Run 2026-11-02T07:55:51+00:00 at `1c9b9b4` (one scored run;

**Fails.** Condition 1 holds; condition 2 does not.

1. WR-10 beats the page, as served and refit, on both metrics over weeks
   1–3 and over weeks 2–3. Over weeks 2–3 it is +5.58 points of pairwise
   and −0.339 MAE against the refit.
2. It does not beat the plain prior. It orders fewer pairs (60.65% vs
   61.16%, −0.51 points) for a slightly lower MAE (3.931 vs 3.944). Each
   week shows the same: fewer pairs in weeks 1, 2 and 3 (−0.23, −0.81 and
   −0.46 points), MAE 0.02 lower in weeks 1 and 2 and level in week 3
   (+0.0006).

The plain prior beats the page on both metrics over weeks 1–3 and weeks
2–3, as it did in 6 of 7 historical folds.

- **Where it lost: first-round rookies** (bands reported, not judged; 10
  player-weeks). WR-10 put 2026's first-round rookie receivers at 8.76
  points a week on average; they scored 4.20 (bias −4.56, MAE 4.56 against
  the prior's 2.15). In every other rookie band its MAE is lower than the
  prior's: 4.95 vs 6.06 in round 2 (n = 5), 2.90 vs 2.95 in rounds 3–4
  (n = 29), 2.69 vs 3.65 in rounds 5–7 (n = 13) and 1.74 vs 2.29 for
  undrafted rookies (n = 7). Veterans are unchanged (4.06 vs 4.08, n = 392).
  Had the bias condition been judged, two bands with n ≥ 10 would be
  outside ±1.5: first-round rookies (−4.56) and last season's 15–20 points
  per game (+4.47, n = 13). The plain prior shares the second miss (+4.53).
- **Sleeper** (context, not the bar) is ahead of every system over weeks
  1–3: 64.80% and 3.522, which is +4.14 points of pairwise and −0.409 MAE
  on WR-10. In week 1 it is 63.07% and 3.732. Only in week 3 do the prior
  and WR-10 order slightly more pairs than it does.

WR-10's frozen fit still gives the page a week 1–3 receiver number it does
not have. But on the untouched season it adds nothing to last season alone.
Draft capital helped the later-round and undrafted rookies and
over-projected the first round, and the plain prior orders more pairs. As
pre-registered, WR-10 is not a contender for the 2027 week 1–3 board as it
stands; a redesign would be a new pre-registration. Nothing changes on the
page, and the DECISIONS.md row comes in the offseason.
