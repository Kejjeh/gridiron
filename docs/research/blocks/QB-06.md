# QB-06: target share of his receivers who will not play this week (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen; the only runs
before this were `--coverage-only` (coverage and the leak check, no score).

## Idea
Roadmap QB-06, "Top receiver out, weighted by target share" (injuries,
usage; workstream C). The shipped QB set carries his own volume and expected
points (`xfp_l3`, `xfp_season`, `xfp_last`), his points over expected, the
implied team total (books move a total when a star receiver is ruled out)
and `vacated_pickup`. `vacated_pickup` hands the Out/Doubtful teammates'
recent opportunities (carries plus targets, last 3 games, every position
pooled) to each active teammate by his share of the team's opportunities. A
quarterback holds few of those (his carries), so at QB it reads as a little
more volume, the wrong sign for a passer who loses a receiver. Nothing in the
set says how much of his passing game will be missing.

## Hypothesis
His attempts barely move when a receiver is out, but completions, yards and
touchdowns fall when the targets go to lesser receivers: the more of the
team's target share is unavailable, the fewer points. The absence is a fact
known before the game, not an in-season efficiency delta (rule #6); the
receivers' target shares are volume, season to date.

## Features (`COLUMNS`)
| column | position(s) | definition, as of the weeks before the row's week | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `vac_tgt_share` | QB | the sum, over his team's players unavailable this week, of each one's target share this season: his targets ÷ his teams' targets, over the games he played for this team before this week | nflverse play-by-play (targets), snap counts and box scores (games played), final injury reports, weekly rosters |
| `vac_tgt_share_new` | QB | the part of that sum from players who played the team's previous game (an absence new this week, which his own recent games do not show yet) | same |

Unavailable (`_p2a_events_offense.py`): Out or Doubtful on the week's final
injury report; or, on the week's weekly roster, a reserve status (RES, EXE,
and the 2019-2020 codes PUP, SUS, NWT, RSN, RSR, E01, E14, RET that 2021 on
folds into RES) or a departure status (CUT, TRD, TRC, UFA, RFA), or listed on
another team only. Never INA (the game-day inactive list, 90 minutes before
kickoff), never DEV, and Questionable is not Out (rule #11). Receivers are
whoever holds targets, any position (rule #4). A share is over the player's
own games for the team, so a receiver on reserve since week 2 counts his
weeks 1-2 share every week he is out.

Play definitions and games played: `_p1a_pbp_roles.py` (a target is a pass
with a receiver named, not a two-point try; it matches the box scores
exactly; his games are offensive snaps or a box score with a carry, target
or pass attempt).

Feature values only (no outcomes): `vac_tgt_share` is above 0 on 79-91% of
QB rows per season (mean 0.14-0.23); `vac_tgt_share_new` on 30-45% (median
0.09-0.12 where above 0). Checked by hand on several team-weeks: the
flagged players are the injured, rested (week 18), traded and released
receivers of that week.

Missing when: never on the scored rows (every team has played by week 4,
and every team-week has a report and a weekly roster). Were a value missing
it would enter as 0 plus `<col>__known`.

Runner bug, worked around: `ctx.injuries` over several seasons that include
2025 returns 2025 only (2025's file adds `season_type`, which
`Context._get` filters on before `game_type`). The helper reads the reports
one season at a time.
