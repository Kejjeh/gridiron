# RB-05: early-down carry share and passing-down target share (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen; the only runs
before this were `--coverage-only` (coverage and the leak check, no score).

## Idea
Roadmap RB-05, "Early-down vs passing-down role", from play-by-play (down,
distance, targets). The shipped RB set has his raw carries (`carries_l3`),
his overall target share (`tgt_share_l3`), expected points, snap share and
the depth-chart rank. It has no carry share at all, and nothing that splits
his work by situation: a committee's early-down back and its third-down back
can show similar opportunities and expected points while holding different
roles.

## Hypothesis
A back's situational role is sticky within a season. His share of the
team's early-down carries says how much of the base offense's run game is
his, normalised for the team's volume (which `carries_l3` is not). His share
of the passing-down targets names the third-down and two-minute back, whose
targets the overall target share mixes with early-down checkdowns. Both are
volume shares, so they update fast (rule #6): season to date and last 3.

## Features (`COLUMNS`)
| column | position | definition, as of the weeks before the row's week | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `early_carry_share_std` | RB | his designed carries on 1st or 2nd down ÷ his teams' designed carries on 1st or 2nd down, over his games this season | nflverse play-by-play, snap counts, box scores |
| `early_carry_share_l3` | RB | the same over his last 3 games | same |
| `pd_tgt_share_std` | RB | his targets on 3rd or 4th down or in a half's last two minutes (`half_seconds_remaining` ≤ 120) ÷ his teams' such targets, over his games this season | same |
| `pd_tgt_share_l3` | RB | the same over his last 3 games | same |

Definitions (`_p1a_pbp_roles.py`): a designed carry is play_type `run`,
`rush_attempt` 1, not a scramble, not a two-point try, not an aborted snap
(kneels never count). A target is play_type `pass`, `pass_attempt` 1, a
receiver named, not a two-point try (it matches the box-score targets
exactly). His games are the games he was on the field for on offense:
offensive snaps > 0 in the snap counts, or a box score with a carry, target or
pass attempt.

One deviation from the roadmap's wording ("the team's RB carries"): the
early-down denominator is every designed carry, whoever ran it. Rule #4: no
position tag decides whose carries count. A quarterback's designed runs and a
receiver's sweeps are carries the back did not get.

Missing when: no earlier game this season (all four), or his teams had no
such plays in those games (the share). Missing enters as 0 plus
`<col>__known`.
