# WR-03: teammate absence redistributed by target share and by last season's route share (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen; the only runs
before this were `--coverage-only` (coverage and the leak check, no score).

## Idea
Roadmap WR-03, "Teammate absence redistributed by route share"
(participation routes as a prior, FTN live). FTN's free charting names no
route runners, and participation (routes: the players on the field for a
dropback, `offense_players`) stops at 2025. So the 2026 board can know only
last season's route share; in-season shares come from targets.

**Overlap with the shipped `vacated_pickup`.** Both start from teammates who
will not play. `vacated_pickup`: Out or Doubtful only; their mean carries
plus targets over their last 3 games; handed to every active teammate, all
positions, by his share of the team's last-3 opportunities. WR-03: Out or
Doubtful, plus reserve lists and departures (weekly rosters); season-to-date
TARGET shares only (carries are not a receiver's to inherit); handed out only
among the players who hold targets, by target share or by route share. Two of
the three columns count only an absence new this week. On a week a receiver
is ruled Out after playing the previous game, both features move together.
`wr_vac_tgt_all` is the closest to `vacated_pickup`, and the two new-absence
columns the furthest from it. The role-change study's `absent_pickup` and
`reserve_pickup` (same position, by last-game share) did not earn a place
(ROLE_CHANGE_BACKTEST.md).

## Hypothesis
The targets a missing receiver leaves go to the receivers who remain, in
proportion to how much of the passing game each already gets (target share)
or how often each is out on a route (route share). The gain is largest in an
absence's first week, before his own last game shows the new role. Volume,
season to date (rule #6); last season's route share is a slow prior.

## Features (`COLUMNS`)
| column | position(s) | definition, as of the weeks before the row's week | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `wr_vac_tgt_new` | WR | V_new × his target share ÷ the summed target shares of his team's receivers still available | nflverse play-by-play, snap counts, box scores, final injury reports, weekly rosters |
| `wr_vac_route_new` | WR | V_new × his route share LAST season ÷ the summed last-season route shares of his team's receivers still available (those with one) | the same, plus participation `offense_players` on last season's dropbacks |
| `wr_vac_tgt_all` | WR | V_all × his target share ÷ the summed target shares of his team's receivers still available | as `wr_vac_tgt_new` |

V_new: the sum, over his team's players unavailable this week who played the
team's previous game, of each one's target share this season (his targets ÷
his teams' targets, over his games for this team). V_all: the same over
every unavailable teammate, however long he has been out. Receivers are
whoever holds targets this season, any position (rule #4). A target share is
over the player's own games for the team.

Route share (`_p2a_events_offense.route_shares`): his routes (dropbacks he
was on the field for, the shipped `+routes` definition,
`A.routes_from_participation`) over his teams' charted dropbacks in the
games he ran one, whole previous regular season, with any team.

Unavailable (`_p2a_events_offense.py`): Out or Doubtful on the week's final
injury report; or, on the week's weekly roster, a reserve status (RES, EXE,
and the 2019-2020 codes PUP, SUS, NWT, RSN, RSR, E01, E14, RET) or a
departure status (CUT, TRD, TRC, UFA, RFA), or listed on another team only.
Never INA, never DEV, and Questionable is not Out (rule #11).

Feature values only (no outcomes): `wr_vac_tgt_new` is above 0 on 28-43% of
WR rows per season (median 0.008-0.011 of the team's targets where above 0,
90th percentile 0.035-0.043, at most 0.24); `wr_vac_tgt_all` on 73-85%.
Checked by hand on 2024 team-weeks: the arithmetic (vacated share × his
share ÷ the remaining receivers' shares), and the largest values belong to
receivers whose team's top target was first out that week, traded, or rested
in week 18.

Missing when: something is vacated but his own weight is undefined: no game
for the team yet (`wr_vac_tgt_new` 0.4-0.9% of WR rows, `wr_vac_tgt_all`
0.9-1.6%), or no route data last season (rookies, or no routes run;
`wr_vac_route_new` 6.6-10.5%). Missing enters as 0 plus `<col>__known`.

Runner bug, worked around: `ctx.injuries` over several seasons that include
2025 returns 2025 only; the helper reads the reports one season at a time.

### Correction 2026-11-02: one bug-fix re-run

The near-miss bug hunt (after round 1, before any round-2 score) found a bug in the shared helper `_p2a_events_offense.roster_away`, which decides who is unavailable for this block's columns. A player missing from his team's weekly roster counted as away only when another team listed him. The 2019 rosters keep a released player as a CUT row for the rest of the season; from 2020 the CUT row appears about once, and a released player nobody signs then has no row anywhere. So from 2020 such a player counted as available, and 2019 and the later folds measured different things. The pre-registration counts a departure as unavailable, so this was a bug in the code, not a definition. Fixed in `fb26c4b`: off his team's roster (while the team has one) means away, in every season. This block's module and the pre-registration above are unchanged.
