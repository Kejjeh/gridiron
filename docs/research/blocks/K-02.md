# K-02: head coach's 4th-down aggressiveness (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen; the only runs
before this were `--coverage-only` (coverage and the leak check, no score).

## Idea
Roadmap K-02, "Coach 4th-down aggressiveness", from play-by-play and the
head-coach rows of the play-caller table (workstream B). A head coach who
goes for it where the league kicks takes field-goal tries away from his
kicker. The shipped kicker set (`K_ADV` in `block_runner.py`) carries the
team's field-goal and extra-point tries per game this season, the kicker's
points (season and last 3), the implied total, the spread and the roof;
nothing about the head coach as a person, and no history from before this
season.

## Hypothesis
Fourth-down aggressiveness is the head coach's habit and follows him to a
new team. An aggressive coach converts some would-be field goals into
attempts to score a touchdown, so his kicker gets fewer field-goal tries
(and some extra points) than the team's tries so far say, most of all in
field-goal range. His career (2018 on) plus this season estimates the habit
better than this season's few 4th downs. Volume (rule #6).

## Features (`COLUMNS`)
| column | position | definition, as of the weeks before the row's week | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `hc_goe` | K | the week's head coach (HC) of the kicker's team: on his 4th-down decisions, went for it (1/0) minus the league's go rate in the same distance and field bucket and season; summed over every earlier season as head coach (2018 on, any team) and this season with this team before the row's week (against the league's bucket rates over this season's earlier weeks), ÷ (his decisions + 50) | nflverse play-by-play 2018–2025, the table's HC rows (nflverse schedules split by sourced in-season changes), the 2018 schedule's coaches |
| `hc_goe_fg` | K | the same over his decisions inside the opponent's 40 (`yardline_100` ≤ 40) only | same |

4th-down decision (`_tendencies.py`, `fourth_obs`): `down` 4 and
`play_type` pass, run, punt or field goal (no-plays and kneels never
count), not a two-point try, not an aborted snap, 0.1 ≤ wp ≤ 0.9,
regulation, not the last two minutes of either half; going for it is a
pass or run (fakes included). Buckets: yards to go (1, 2–3, 4–6, 7+) ×
`yardline_100` (1–10, 11–25, 26–40, 41–60, 61–99). 2019–2025: 46–115
decisions a team-season (mean 77), 16–44 inside the 40 (mean 27); 2018 adds
2,344 decisions to the head coaches' history. The league's go rate on these
decisions rose from 0.09 (2018) to 0.17 (2025), which is why each decision
is measured against its own season's bucket rate. The 50 decisions of
shrinkage were set before any score (a 0/1 decision's noise against a
plausible spread of about 5 points of go rate between coaches).

Who is in charge: the person the table names as HC for (season, team,
week): every team-week of 2019–2025 has one (3,070 of 3,070 team-games of
weeks 4–18). 2018 head coaches come from the pre-game schedule, which
splits that season's two in-season changes. 82 people were head coaches in
2019–2025, 8 of them for more than one team.

Missing when: he has neither an earlier season as head coach (2018 on) nor
an earlier game this season with this team, which is an interim or new
coach's first game in charge. Missing enters as 0 plus `<col>__known`.
