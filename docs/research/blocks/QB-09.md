# QB-09: QB sneaks per game, all and inside the opponent's 5 (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen; the only runs
before this were `--coverage-only` (coverage and the leak check, no score).

## Idea
Roadmap QB-09, "Goal-line sneak usage", from FTN charting's `is_qb_sneak`
(the free nflverse subset, 2022 on, live in 2026). The shipped QB set
already carries expected fantasy points (`xfp_l3`, `xfp_season`,
`xfp_last`: ff_opportunity prices every carry by its down, distance and
field position), carries (`carries_l3`), points over expected
(`fpoe_season`), last game's opportunities (`opp_last`) and the implied team
total. None of them knows that a carry was a sneak. RB-03 (a back's share of
the carries inside the 5) failed in 0 of 7 folds: a goal-line role that
expected points already price added nothing. This tests a narrower role
that expected points cannot see, the called sneak.

## Hypothesis
A quarterback whose team calls the sneak (near the goal line, the tush
push) converts short-yardage and goal-line carries more often than a generic
quarterback carry from the same spot, so expected points under-price him and
he scores more rushing touchdowns than they say. The sneak is a play-calling
habit that holds through a season: volume (rule #6), season to date.

## Features (`COLUMNS`)
| column | position(s) | definition, as of the weeks before the row's week | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sneaks_pg` | QB | his QB sneaks (FTN `is_qb_sneak` on a play-by-play `play_type == "run"` with him as the rusher; two-point tries and no-plays excluded) per FTN-charted game he played this season | FTN charting joined to play-by-play on (`game_id`, `play_id`); player box scores for his games |
| `gl_sneaks_pg` | QB | the same, only sneaks snapped inside the opponent's 5 (`yardline_100 <= 5`), per charted game | same |

Missing when: he has no earlier game this season, or none of his earlier
games was charted, which is every row of 2019-2021 (FTN starts in 2022).
Missing enters as 0 plus `<col>__known`.

Shared code: `scripts/research/blocks/_p1b_ftn_pfr_opponent.py`
(`ftn_plays`, `charted_history`). Under the block's filters FTN charts
286, 305, 283 and 305 regular-season sneaks in 2022-2025, of which 47, 66,
53 and 58 were inside the 5. The feature is sparse by nature: of the 2,038
QB rows with a value (2022-2025), 37% have no sneak to date and 73% no
goal-line sneak.
