# TE-06: change of quarterback this week, tight ends (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen; the only runs
before this were `--coverage-only` (coverage and the leak check, no score).

## Idea
Roadmap TE-06, "QB-change effect" (workstream C; kind A; "Shares WR-02").
The adjustment is naturally a feature (the change, its direction, and the
tight end's games with the incoming quarterback), so it is tested as a
feature block, with WR-02's definition (`_p2a_events_offense.qb_change`) on
tight-end rows.

The shipped TE set carries the implied team total (books move a total for a
quarterback change), his target share, air-yards share and expected points
(made with the quarterback of those games), his points over expected and
`vacated_pickup` (which counts a quarterback ruled out only through his
carries). Nothing in it says the quarterback changes this week.

## Hypothesis
When the quarterback changes, a tight end's recent production was made with
someone else. A quarterback who has thrown less of the team's passes this
season (a backup) lowers it; a returning starter restores it. A tight end
who has already played with the incoming quarterback keeps more of his
role. A backup's short, safe throws may favour the tight end over the wide
receivers, so the effect need not match WR-02's. An availability fact and
volume (rule #6), not an efficiency delta.

## Features (`COLUMNS`)
| column | position(s) | definition, as of the weeks before the row's week | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `qb_change` | TE | 1 when this week's expected starter is not the QB with the most dropbacks in the team's previous game; else 0 | pre-game depth charts, final injury reports, weekly rosters, nflverse play-by-play |
| `qb_share_delta` | TE | the expected starter's share of the team's dropbacks this season (before this week) minus that previous-game QB's share; 0 when they are the same (negative: a QB who has thrown less of the team's passes comes in) | nflverse play-by-play |
| `games_with_qb` | TE | his games for the team this season, before this week, in which the expected starter had the team's most dropbacks | nflverse play-by-play, snap counts, box scores |

Expected starter (`_p2a_events_offense.py`): the first QB on the team's
pre-game depth chart for the week (2025: the latest snapshot labelled for the
week; a week with no chart uses the latest earlier one) who is not
unavailable: not Out or Doubtful on the week's final injury report, not on a
reserve list or departed per the week's roster, not listed on another team
only. Checked before any score against who took the team's first dropback:
it names him in 92.6% of 3,742 team-games, 2019-2025 (89.7-96.5% per
season); the chart's QB1 alone, in 87.7%. A dropback is `qb_dropback` 1, not
a two-point try, credited to `passer_id` (scrambles included).

Feature values only (no outcomes): `qb_change` is 1 on 6-13% of TE rows per
season. `qb_share_delta` where it is not 0: a median of -0.44 to +0.09 by
season, from -1.00 to +0.87. Some changes are the chart's errors: on the 7%
of team-games where the expected starter is not the one who starts, the
feature says change when there is none, or the reverse.

Missing when: the chart lists no available QB (0.4% of TE rows in 2021, 0.3%
in 2023, none in other seasons). Missing enters as 0 plus `<col>__known`.

Runner bugs, worked around: `ctx.depth_charts` over several seasons that
include 2025 drops every 2025 row (the timestamped layout has no
`game_type`), and `ctx.injuries` over several seasons that include 2025
drops 2019-2024. The helper reads both one season at a time.
