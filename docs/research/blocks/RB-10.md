# RB-10: running backs new to the team's 53 this week, on the other backs (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen; the only runs
before this were `--coverage-only` (coverage and the leak check, no score)
and counts of how often each column is nonzero (no actual points read).

## Idea
Roadmap RB-10, "Role-at-risk flag from transactions" (kind A, workstream C,
record-only first), cast as features on every running back of the team: a
new running back on the team's 53 this week that was not there in its
previous game. The weekly rosters carry every kind of arrival by gsis id: a
signing, a waiver claim, a trade (a traded back is new on his new team's
roster, so nflverse's trades table, keyed by PFR id and date, adds nothing a
roster comparison does not have), a call-up from the practice squad, an
activation from an injury list. Sleeper's player map, the roadmap's other
source, exists for 2026 only, so it cannot be tested. The shipped RB set
carries his own usage (`xfp_*`, `carries_l3`, `snap_*`, `opp_last`), his
depth-chart rank and `vacated_pickup`; none of them knows a back arrived
this week.

## Hypothesis
When a team puts a new running back on its 53, the backs already there lose
some of the work, more when the arrival has carried a real workload
elsewhere or before his absence. The team's choice to add a back is news its
incumbents' averages do not hold. Volume (rule #6).

## Features (`COLUMNS`)
| column | position(s) | definition, as of the weeks before the row's week | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `rb_new_ext` | RB | running backs (roster position RB) on the team's 53 this week (status ACT or INA, INA read as ACT) who were on none of its lists in its previous game week: signings, claims, trades. Never counts himself | weekly rosters |
| `rb_new_int` | RB | running backs on the team's 53 this week who were with the team in its previous game week but not on its 53: called up from its practice squad, activated from a reserve list. Never counts himself | weekly rosters |
| `rb_new_opp` | RB | the sum, over the backs counted in the two columns above, of their carries + targets per game over their last 8 games before this week (any team, this season and last; 0 for an arrival with none) | weekly rosters, box scores |

Missing when: the team has no roster this week or none in an earlier week
this season (never on these rows, which start in week 4). It would enter as
0 plus `__known`.

Shared code: `scripts/research/blocks/_p2b_events_ramps_defense.py`
(`roster`, `box`). The weekly roster is one snapshot per team game, none in
a bye week, so "previous game week" is the team's previous roster.

On the 8,412 RB rows of 2019-2025: `rb_new_ext` is nonzero on 180 rows
(2019 38, 2020 12, 2021 38, 2022 38, 2023 23, 2024 9, 2025 22; 13 of them
with two arrivals). `rb_new_int` is nonzero on 993 rows (51 in 2019, then
124-225 a season once practice-squad elevations began in 2020).
`rb_new_opp` is nonzero on 832 rows, at most 25.75.
