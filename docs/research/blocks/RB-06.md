# RB-06: a teammate back's early-down, passing-down and goal-line role, vacated this week (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen; the only runs
before this were `--coverage-only` (coverage and the leak check, no score).

## Idea
Roadmap RB-06, "Vacated carries redistributed by role" (injuries,
play-by-play; workstream C: "early-down, passing-down and red-zone work,
replacing the single vacated-opportunity share"). The shipped RB set carries
`vacated_pickup`: the Out/Doubtful teammates' opportunities (carries plus
targets, mean of their last 3 games, every position pooled), handed to every
active teammate by his share of the team's opportunities. A backup back holds
about 5% of them, so a starter's 18 vacated opportunities count about 1 for
him, and a receiver's absence counts for him too, by the same rule. The set
also carries this week's depth-chart rank (which may not yet promote him) and
his last-game usage (`xfp_last`, `opp_last`, `snap_last`; at RB the largest fast-moving
weight, ROLE_CHANGE_BACKTEST.md), which prices a new role from the absence's
second week on. Earlier tests: the role-change block's `absent_pickup` and
`reserve_pickup` (same position, handed out by last-game share) did not earn
a place, and RB-05 (the back's own early-down and passing-down shares)
failed (better on both metrics in 4 of 7 folds).

## Hypothesis
In the first week a back is out, his work goes to the other backs, role by
role: the early-down carries to the backs who already get early-down
carries, the passing-down targets to the receiving back, the goal-line
carries to whoever has had them. A back picks up each role in proportion to
his share of it among the backs still available. Volume, season-to-date
shares (rule #6).

## Features (`COLUMNS`)
| column | position(s) | definition, as of the weeks before the row's week | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `rb_vac_early` | RB | V × (his share) ÷ (the summed shares of his team's backs still available), for designed carries on 1st and 2nd down | nflverse play-by-play, snap counts, box scores, final injury reports, weekly rosters |
| `rb_vac_pd` | RB | the same for targets on 3rd or 4th down or in a half's last two minutes (`half_seconds_remaining` ≤ 120) | same |
| `rb_vac_gl` | RB | the same for designed carries from the opponent's 5 or closer (`yardline_100` ≤ 5) | same |

V, for each role: the sum, over his team's backs who are unavailable this
week AND played the team's previous game, of each one's season-to-date share
of the team's plays of that kind (his count ÷ his teams' count, over his
games for this team). A back's share is the same quotient for him.
"Back" is the box-score position (RB, FB) of the player's latest game for
the team; how much of each role he holds is usage (rule #4).

Unavailable (`_p2a_events_offense.py`): Out or Doubtful on the week's final
injury report; or, on the week's weekly roster, a reserve status (RES, EXE,
and the 2019-2020 codes PUP, SUS, NWT, RSN, RSR, E01, E14, RET) or a
departure status (CUT, TRD, TRC, UFA, RFA), or listed on another team only.
Never INA, never DEV, and Questionable is not Out (rule #11).

One deviation from the roadmap text ("when a teammate RB is
Out/Doubtful/reserve this week"): only an absence new this week counts (he
played the team's previous game). From the second week on, the backup's own
last game shows the role, and the shipped set prices it.

Play definitions (`_p1a_pbp_roles.py`, as RB-05 and RB-03 count them): a
designed carry is play_type `run`, `rush_attempt` 1, not a scramble, not a
two-point try, not an aborted snap; a target is a pass with a receiver
named, not a two-point try.

Feature values only (no outcomes): `rb_vac_early` is above 0 on 7-13% of RB
rows per season (median 0.06-0.13 of the team's early-down carries where
above 0, 90th percentile 0.34-0.50); `rb_vac_pd` on 3-9%; `rb_vac_gl` on
2-6%. Checked by hand on 2023: the largest values are the backups in the
first week their team's lead back was out.

Missing when: a back is vacated but this back's own share is undefined (no
game for the team yet, or his games had no team plays of that kind), or no
remaining back holds any of that role (S = 0): 0.1-0.7% of RB rows
(`rb_vac_early`), 0.4-1.0% (`rb_vac_pd`), 0.8-2.0% (`rb_vac_gl`). Missing
enters as 0 plus `<col>__known`.

Runner bug, worked around: `ctx.injuries` over several seasons that include
2025 returns 2025 only; the helper reads the reports one season at a time.

### Correction 2026-11-02: one bug-fix re-run

The near-miss bug hunt (after round 1, before any round-2 score) found a bug in the shared helper `_p2a_events_offense.roster_away`, which decides who is unavailable for this block's columns. A player missing from his team's weekly roster counted as away only when another team listed him. The 2019 rosters keep a released player as a CUT row for the rest of the season; from 2020 the CUT row appears about once, and a released player nobody signs then has no row anywhere. So from 2020 such a player counted as available, and 2019 and the later folds measured different things. The pre-registration counts a departure as unavailable, so this was a bug in the code, not a definition. Fixed in `fb26c4b`: off his team's roster (while the team has one) means away, in every season. This block's module and the pre-registration above are unchanged.

Values the fix changes (block columns, all 2019-2025 rows; no scores):

- `rb_vac_early`: 34 of 8,412 rows (2021 13, 2022 13, 2023 5, 2025 3)
- `rb_vac_gl`: 3 of 8,412 rows (2022 1, 2023 2)
- `rb_vac_pd`: 11 of 8,412 rows (2019 2, 2021 1, 2022 5, 2023 3)

Coverage is unchanged at the stated precision.
