# RB-13-2026: RB-13's frozen pipeline on 2026 weeks 4–7 (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen. The only runs
before this were `--holdout-2026 --coverage-only` (the 2026 coverage, the
window check, the leak check and the reproduction check, no 2026 score) and
a plumbing test of the scoring code in which every 2026 actual was replaced
by a random draw before anything read it. That test wrote nothing into the
repo.

## Idea
RB-13 passed its historical bar (`results/RB-13.json`: lower CRPS than the
page's normal in 7 of 7 folds, and a pooled P(win) Brier not worse; see
`RB-13.md`). With 78 hypotheses, some pass the historical bar by luck
(ground rule 3). The 2026 season is the untouched holdout, as `ROUND2.md`
part 2 and `WR-10-2026.md` used it. RB-13 was designed and scored on
2019–2025 only. Its result was committed before this test was written, and
no run has scored a 2026 row of it.

A review of the P7 results found that for P(win) alone a refitted, wider
normal does as well as RB-13 (their Briers matched). This test does not
change RB-13. It asks whether its historical result holds on new data.

## Hypothesis
The pipeline that beat the page's normal in every historical fold also beats
it on 2026: a lower mean CRPS on the running-back rows of weeks 4–7, and a
P(win) Brier that is not worse. The cause would be the same: a back's week
is six-point lumps on top of a skewed yardage total, and one normal cannot
follow that shape.

## The window (fixed before any 2026 score)
Rule: a 2026 week counts only when every scheduled game of it is in the
table. That is, every regular-season game in the 2026 schedule has its box
scores in the cached 2026 season table and both its defenses in the DEF
table. The cached tables come from `holdout_2026.py` (`table_2026`, built
2026-11-02 at 01:38 UTC, digest `235263ec7de3d2c7`); this test does not
rebuild them.

| 2026 week | games scheduled | in the table | counts |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 16 | 16 | yes |
| 5 | 15 | 15 | yes |
| 6 | 14 | 14 | yes |
| 7 | 14 | 14 | yes |
| 8 | 14 | 12 | no: its Sunday-night game is missing and its Monday game is tonight |

So the window is 2026 weeks 4–7. Weeks 1–3 are outside the harness, which
starts at week 4 as in history. The window does not grow. Later weeks belong
to the record-only check (ground rule 3), not to this test. The harness
holds the four weeks as a constant (`HOLDOUT_WEEKS`) and refuses a run if
any of them is not complete. Only window rows are scored or dealt: the
block is built for them, the means are predicted for them, and every
league is dealt from them. Their inputs use the 2026 games before each
row's week, as in history.

## Test
- Harness: `scripts/research/distribution_backtest.py --holdout-2026` (its
  docstring's "2026 HOLDOUT" section). It is the historical fold code
  (`run_folds`), called once with 2026 as the test season. Without the flag
  the harness runs as before: re-run through the changed harness, the stored
  2025 fold of RB-13, TE-10 and K-03 gives every number in its results JSON.
- The frozen pipeline: RB-13's module, `blocks/_p7_distributions.py` and
  the fold code, unchanged. All of 2019–2025 plays the role of training,
  exactly as in a historical test fold:
  - μ for every 2026 row of every position: the shipped ridge (the shipped
    columns, lambda 5; K: `K_ADV`; DEF: `DEF_ADV`) fit on all of 2019–2025,
    floored at 0 except for DEF;
  - μ for each training row (the 2019–2025 RB rows): models fit on the six
    seasons other than its own (nested), so (a, b) are fitted on
    out-of-sample means like the ones they are scored on;
  - RB-13's (a, b): fitted on all 2019–2025 RB rows by minimum mean CRPS
    (Nelder-Mead from (1.0, 0.4), a in [0.05, 8], b in [0, 1.5]);
  - the fitted normal N(μ, a + bμ): fitted the same way on the same rows;
  - RB-13's 2026 inputs: its own `build` (2026 `ff_opportunity` before each
    row's week; 2025 for the prior). The page's sd: the 2026 table's `sd`.
- Rows: the 2026 RB rows of weeks 4–7 in block_runner.py's universe
  (players Sleeper projected): 311.
- Comparator: the page's normal, N(μ, max(0.55 × baseline, 1.5)).
- Bar, decided now: RB-13's historical bar, applied once and pooled over the
  window. Both must hold, decided on unrounded numbers:
  1. RB-13's mean CRPS over the 311 rows is lower than the page's normal's.
  2. The pooled P(win) Brier over the window's synthetic leagues is not
     worse than the page's (≤).

  The leagues are built as in history: `pwin_calibration.py`'s 12-team
  leagues (its dealing, seeds and slot pools, checked against its own
  `week_matchups`), dealt from the window's rows of every position with the
  frozen means, 32 seeded leagues a week. That is 768 matchups over weeks
  4–7; a tied margin is skipped. Running backs (both RB slots and any back
  dealt to FLEX) carry RB-13; every other starter keeps the page's normal.
  P(win) is computed exactly on the lattice. The week-clustered bootstrap of
  the Brier change is reported as in history. With four weeks it has four
  clusters, so its interval is crude.
- Reported, never the bar:
  - the fitted normal refit on 2019–2025: its CRPS and its P(win) Brier;
  - pinball loss at 10%, 50% and 90%; log loss;
  - for each system, the share of actuals inside its 10–90% interval,
    read against that system's own share of the interval: 80% for a normal,
    and for RB-13 the mass its lattice puts on its closed [q10, q90];
  - every number by week;
  - the frozen-fit parameters, the rows and the matchups used.
- Refusals: a scored run refuses unless this doc, RB-13's module, every
  helper, the harness and `results/RB-13.json` are committed and unchanged;
  the 2019–2025 tables have the digest RB-13 was scored on
  (`42693a4a57591b37`); every window week is complete; the leak check
  passes at 2026 weeks 4, 5, 6 and 7; and the stored 2025 fold, re-run
  through the unchanged fold code on today's data, gives every number in
  `results/RB-13.json`. One scored run. A second run is legitimate only to
  fix a bug, and the Result section would say why.
- Coverage seen before registering (no scores):
  - window: weeks 4–7 complete (16, 15, 14 and 14 games); week 8 has 12 of
    14 and is left out
  - RB rows: 311 (weeks 4, 5, 6, 7: 87, 80, 73, 71)
  - `td_share_std`: week 4 92%, week 5 91%, week 6 92%, week 7 90%
  - `td_games_std`: the same; `td_share_prior`: 100% every week
  - league rows by position (weeks 4/5/6/7): QB 32/32/28/31, RB
    87/80/73/71, WR 142/128/128/122, TE 76/80/68/67, K 29/27/24/24, DEF
    32/30/28/28; 768 matchups dealt
  - leak check: passed (2026 weeks 4, 5, 6 and 7)
  - training tables digest `42693a4a57591b37`, the one RB-13 was scored
    on; 2026 tables digest `235263ec7de3d2c7`
  - reproduction: the stored 2025 fold, re-run, gives every number in

## What a pass means
A pass makes RB-13 a contender, nothing more. It still runs record-only for
two live weeks after this result is committed (ground rule 3) before any
serving decision. Nothing serves after the week-14 board (ground rule 8).
The page would keep its mean and its start/sit order; the distribution
would feed the ± and P(win). If the fitted normal does as well on P(win),
the cheaper change for P(win) is the page's sd, a separate decision.

A fail means RB-13 is not used as it stands. A redesign would be a new
pre-registration. Either way, RB-13's DECISIONS.md row cites this result.

## Result

pre-registration, RB-13's module and the helpers are those of `39813d8`;
the two commits between changed other files. The helpers' combined blob

fixed three other helpers. RB-13 reads only `_p7_distributions.py` and,

**PASS** on the pre-registered bar. Both halves hold, on unrounded numbers:
1. CRPS: 2.8939 against the page's 3.0252, a change of −0.1313 (4.3%
   lower). In history the change was −0.172 a fold on average (5.6%
   pooled). RB-13 is lower in all four weeks.
2. P(win): the pooled Brier is 0.241653 against 0.241976, a change of
   −0.000322 (week-clustered 95% interval −0.000757 to −0.000033; 98.7% of
   the bootstrap draws not worse). With four weeks the interval is crude.
   RB-13 is lower in weeks 4, 6 and 7 and higher in week 5.

Reported, never the bar:
- The review's point holds on 2026. For P(win) the fitted normal is close
  to RB-13: its Brier (0.241666) takes 96% of RB-13's gain over the page.
  For CRPS the shape adds more: RB-13 is 0.064 below the fitted normal
  pooled, and below it in weeks 4, 5 and 6, not in week 7.
- Pinball loss at 10/50/90%: RB-13 0.631 / 2.034 / 1.166, the page 0.693 /
  2.099 / 1.251, the fitted normal 0.695 / 2.099 / 1.171. Log loss: RB-13
  0.676571, the page 0.677342, the fitted normal 0.676606.
- The 10–90% interval, read against each system's own share of it: the
  page's normal holds 68.8% of the actuals against its 80%. RB-13 holds
  80.4% against the 83.5% its lattice puts on its closed interval, about
  3 points short. The fitted normal holds 82.6% against 80%. RB-13's sd
  averages 5.42 points, the page's 4.09.

Next, as pre-registered: RB-13 is a contender, nothing more. It runs
