# QB-12: week 1–3 prior from last season, with a head-coach change and receiver turnover (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen. The only runs
before this were `--coverage-only` (coverage, feature counts and the leak
check, no score) and a dry run of the scoring code on random synthetic
outcomes (no real points).

## Idea
Roadmap QB-12, "Week 1–3 prior: last season, new coordinator, receiver
turnover" (kind A, phase 4, for 2027; it overlaps `returner_v2`). It is
tested now on 2019–2025, so the offseason only re-tests it on 2026.

What the page does in weeks 1–3 today: the advanced model and the stack
start in week 4 (`min_week`). Before that the page serves
`gridiron.projection` on this season's box scores alone. In week 1 it
abstains for every player. In weeks 2–3 it projects a player who has an
earlier game from one or two games of usage, shrunk to role priors, and
abstains on the rest. Nothing on the page reads last season, a coaching
change or the receivers a team lost. The shipped set (expected points,
shares, depth chart) does not run in these weeks, so the only overlap is the
page's own in-season number, and every system below carries it.

This is not a runner block: `block_runner.py` scores weeks 4–18. Its
harness is `scripts/research/early_season_backtest.py`, and the script's
docstring holds the shared method. The roadmap row says "coordinator". This
test uses the head coach instead, because the nflverse schedules carry him
for every game (`home_coach`, `away_coach`) and he is reliable for offseason
changes. Coordinator changes are left to the play-caller table (workstream B).

## Hypothesis
Last season's points per game, discounted, is the best single number for a
quarterback before his own games pile up: the page has nothing in week 1 and
only one or two games after it. A quarterback whose head coach changed (a
new team counts) scores less early on than last season says, while the new
system settles. So does one whose team lost much of last season's target
volume. Both are offseason facts, fixed before week 1. Last season's points
are a full-season prior; nothing about this season's efficiency is read
(rule #6). The bar does not test signs; the per-unit coefficients of the
frozen fit are reported.

## Features
| column | position | definition, as of before the row's week | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `page` | QB | the page's number today: `gridiron.projection.project` on this season's box scores before the week, with the week's implied total; missing where it abstains | box scores, schedules (lines) |
| `ppg_prev` | QB | his league points per game over last season's regular-season games (any team); missing with fewer than 4 games | box scores |
| `new_coach` | QB | 1 if his team's head coach in its first game this season is not the head coach of most of his games last season (on a tie, the later game's coach), else 0; a team change counts | schedules (`home_coach`, `away_coach`), box scores |
| `tgt_left` | QB | the share of his team's regular-season targets last season that went to players who are not on its week-1 roster this season with status ACT or INA (left, cut, practice squad, a reserve list) | box scores, weekly rosters (week 1) |

Missing when: `page` in week 1, and for a player with no earlier game this
season; `ppg_prev` with fewer than 4 games last season; `new_coach` with no
game last season (a rookie, a year out). `tgt_left` is never missing.
Missing enters as 0 plus `<col>__known`.

## Test
- Position: QB. Rows: every week 1–3 player-week of 2019–2025 with a box
  score and a Sleeper projection: 784 rows, 652 of them with Sleeper 5+.
- Systems, each fitted separately for weeks 1, 2 and 3 on the training
  seasons (`A.Ridge`, lam 5), with fitted predictions floored at 0:
  - `null`: the training mean.
  - `today`: the page as served, or the null where it abstains.
  - `today_fit`: [page].
  - `prior`: [page, ppg_prev], last season discounted.
  - `cand`: prior plus [new_coach, tgt_left].

  Sleeper's projection is context only, never part of the bar.
- Seasons: 2019–2025, seven season folds; every other season trains.
- Bar (all three must hold):
  1. `cand` beats both `today` and `today_fit` on MAE and on start/sit
     pairwise (within a week, among Sleeper 5+) in every fold. This must
     hold over weeks 1–3 and again over weeks 2–3 alone.
  2. `cand` beats `prior` on MAE and pairwise in every fold (weeks 1–3):
     the coach change and the turnover add something.
  3. Out-of-fold `cand`, 2019–2025 pooled: |bias| ≤ 1.5 points in every band
     with n ≥ 10. The bands are last season's points per game (none, <5,
     5–10, 10–15, 15–20, 20+), `new_coach` (same, new, none) and `tgt_left`
     (<25%, 25–50%, 50%+).

  `prior` is judged on conditions 1 and 3 as well, and reported as
  `prior_clears`. That result is QB-12's first clause on its own; it is not
  a pass for QB-12.
- Coverage seen before registering (no scores):

## If it passes
The frozen fit (`frozen_fit` in the JSON, fitted on all seven seasons) is
applied unchanged to 2026 weeks 1–3 in the offseason (phase 4). Nothing has
looked at 2026 weeks 1–3 with it. Only if it holds there does it get a
DECISIONS.md row and a place on the 2027 week 1–3 board. Nothing changes in
2026.
