# WR-08: team pass volume implied by the play-caller (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen; the only runs
before this were `--coverage-only` (coverage and the leak check, no score).

## Idea
Roadmap WR-08, "Volume from play-caller pass rate over expected and pace",
workstream B (shares its tendencies with QB-03 and QB-04). A receiver's
targets are his share of his team's passes times the passes. The shipped WR
set carries his share (target and air-yards share over the last 3 games),
his recent volume (expected points, last game) and the implied team total;
nothing about the pass volume the person calling the plays implies, and no
history from before this season. A linear model cannot multiply his share
by the team's implied volume from separate columns, so the block gives the
product.

## Hypothesis
A play-caller who passes more than expected and plays fast produces more
pass plays than his offense's last few games show, and the extra passes go
to receivers in proportion to their shares. His career plus this season
estimates the habit better than one team's recent games. Volume (rule #6).

## Features (`COLUMNS`)
| column | position | definition, as of the weeks before the row's week | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `pc_pass_volume` | WR | the week's play-caller (OPC) for the receiver's team: (1 + proe ÷ (100 × r)) × (s ÷ (s + pace)), where proe is QB-03's pooled neutral pass rate over expected (his career plus this season with this team, ÷ (plays + 100)), pace is QB-04's `pc_pace` (seconds between snaps over the league, ÷ (pairs + 50)), r the league's neutral pass rate and s the league's mean seconds between snaps, both over the season before the row's season. 1.0 is an average play-caller. | nflverse play-by-play 2018–2025 (2018 for the 2019 constants only), the play-caller table |
| `wr_volume_tilt` | WR | his targets ÷ his teams' targets over the games he played this season, × (`pc_pass_volume` − 1): his part of the extra (or missing) pass volume | the above, nflverse play-by-play, snap counts, box scores |

Definitions (`_tendencies.py`): neutral plays and snap pairs as in QB-03
and QB-04; each tendency measured against the league in the same season
(this season's against the league over this season's earlier weeks). The
pooled pass rate over expected uses both parts, career and this season:
(career deviations + this season's) ÷ (career plays + this season's + 100).
The league's neutral pass rate was 0.569–0.590 a season (2018–2025) and its
mean seconds between snaps 38.0–38.7. A target is a play-by-play pass
attempt (`play_type` pass) with a receiver named, not a two-point try. His
games are the games he was on the field for on offense (offensive snaps
> 0 in the snap counts through the one crosswalk, or a box score with a
carry, target or pass attempt).

Who is in charge: as in QB-03 (the table's OPC; no row means unknown).

Missing when: the team-week has no named play-caller, or he has neither an
earlier season as play-caller nor an earlier game this season with this
team; `wr_volume_tilt` also when the receiver has no earlier game this
season or his teams threw no target in those games. Missing enters as 0
plus `<col>__known`.
