# K-03-2026: K-03's frozen pipeline on 2026 weeks 4–7 (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen. The only runs
before this were `--holdout-2026 --coverage-only` (the 2026 coverage, the
window check, the leak check and the reproduction check, no 2026 score) and
a plumbing test of the scoring code in which every 2026 actual was replaced
by a random draw before anything read it. That test wrote nothing into the
repo.

## Idea
K-03 passed its historical bar (`results/K-03.json`: lower CRPS than the
page's normal in 7 of 7 folds, and a pooled P(win) Brier not worse; see
`K-03.md`). With 78 hypotheses, some pass the historical bar by luck
(ground rule 3). The 2026 season is the untouched holdout, as `ROUND2.md`
part 2 and `WR-10-2026.md` used it. K-03 was designed and scored on
2019–2025 only. Its result was committed before this test was written, and
no run has scored a 2026 row of it.

A review of the P7 results found that most of K-03's CRPS gain comes from a
refitted, nearly flat kicker sd (about 4.4 to 4.5 points) on whole points,
and that for P(win) a refitted normal does as well. This test does not
change K-03. It asks whether its historical result holds on new data, and
it reports the review's cheaper alternative beside it.

## Hypothesis
The pipeline that beat the page's normal in every historical fold also beats
it on 2026: a lower mean CRPS on the kicker rows of weeks 4–7, and a P(win)
Brier that is not worse. The cause would be the same: a kicker's week is a
sum of a few lumpy events on whole points.

## The window (fixed before any 2026 score)
Rule: a 2026 week counts only when every scheduled game of it is in the
table. That is, every regular-season game in the 2026 schedule has its box
scores in the cached 2026 season table and both its defenses in the DEF
table. The cached tables come from `holdout_2026.py` (`table_2026`, built
2026-11-02 at 01:38 UTC, digest `235263ec7de3d2c7`); this test does not
rebuild them.

| 2026 week | games scheduled | in the table | counts |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 16 | 16 | yes |
| 5 | 15 | 15 | yes |
| 6 | 14 | 14 | yes |
| 7 | 14 | 14 | yes |
| 8 | 14 | 12 | no: its Sunday-night game is missing and its Monday game is tonight |

So the window is 2026 weeks 4–7. Weeks 1–3 are outside the harness, which
starts at week 4 as in history. The window does not grow. Later weeks belong
to the record-only check (ground rule 3), not to this test. The harness
holds the four weeks as a constant (`HOLDOUT_WEEKS`) and refuses a run if
any of them is not complete. Only window rows are scored or dealt: the
block is built for them, the means are predicted for them, and every
league is dealt from them. Their inputs use the 2026 games before each
row's week, as in history.

The kicker table keeps the kickers Sleeper projected, as in history. In
week 5 neither kicker of one game had a Sleeper projection, so that game is
in the table but has no kicker row in the universe.

## Test
- Harness: `scripts/research/distribution_backtest.py --holdout-2026` (its
  docstring's "2026 HOLDOUT" section). It is the historical fold code
  (`run_folds`), called once with 2026 as the test season. Without the flag
  the harness runs as before: re-run through the changed harness, the stored
  2025 fold of RB-13, TE-10 and K-03 gives every number in its results JSON.
- The frozen pipeline: K-03's module, `blocks/_p7_distributions.py` and the
  fold code, unchanged. All of 2019–2025 plays the role of training,
  exactly as in a historical test fold:
  - μ for every 2026 row of every position: the shipped ridge (the shipped
    columns, lambda 5; K: `K_ADV`; DEF: `DEF_ADV`) fit on all of 2019–2025,
    floored at 0 except for DEF;
  - μ for each training row (the 2019–2025 kicker rows): models fit on the
    six seasons other than its own (nested);
  - K-03's dispersion κ: one value, fitted on all 2019–2025 kicker rows by
    minimum mean CRPS over the grid {−1/10, −1/12, −1/15, −1/20, −1/30,
    −1/50, 0, 0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.35, 0.5};
  - the fitted normal N(μ, a + bμ): (a, b) by minimum CRPS on the same rows;
  - K-03's 2026 inputs: its own `build` (2026 team and player box scores
    before each row's week; 2025 for the league priors). The page's sd:
    the 2026 table's `sd`.
- Rows: the 2026 kicker rows of weeks 4–7 in block_runner.py's kicker table
  (Sleeper projected, actual known): 104.
- Comparator: the page's normal, N(μ, max(0.55 × baseline, 1.5)).
- Bar, decided now: K-03's historical bar, applied once and pooled over the
  window. Both must hold, decided on unrounded numbers:
  1. K-03's mean CRPS over the 104 rows is lower than the page's normal's.
  2. The pooled P(win) Brier over the window's synthetic leagues is not
     worse than the page's (≤).

  The leagues are built as in history: `pwin_calibration.py`'s 12-team
  leagues (its dealing, seeds and slot pools, checked against its own
  `week_matchups`), dealt from the window's rows of every position with the
  frozen means, 32 seeded leagues a week. That is 768 matchups over weeks
  4–7; a tied margin is skipped. Kickers carry K-03; every other starter
  keeps the page's normal. P(win) is computed exactly on the lattice. The
  week-clustered bootstrap of the Brier change is reported as in history.
  With four weeks it has four clusters, so its interval is crude.

  In history the Brier half was a tie: an unrounded change of −0.0000002
  over 18,811 matchups. One kicker a side barely moves a matchup, so on 768
  matchups this half can fall either way by a hair. The rule does not
  change: not worse means ≤ on unrounded numbers.
- Reported, never the bar:
  - the fitted normal refit on 2019–2025: its CRPS and its P(win) Brier;
  - the page's normal on whole points, as in history (the page's mean and
    sd on the integer lattice);
  - the refitted normal on whole points, the review's cheaper alternative:
    the fitted normal's sd, a + bμ with (a, b) fitted on 2019–2025, on the
    integer lattice. The review found b = 0.00 to 0.06 in the historical
    folds, so this sd is nearly constant;
  - pinball loss at 10%, 50% and 90%; log loss;
  - for each system, the share of actuals inside its 10–90% interval,
    read against that system's own share of the interval: 80% for a
    continuous normal, and for a whole-point distribution (K-03 and the two
    normals on whole points) the mass it puts on its closed [q10, q90];
  - every number by week;
  - the frozen-fit parameters, the rows and the matchups used.
- Refusals: a scored run refuses unless this doc, K-03's module, every
  helper, the harness and `results/K-03.json` are committed and unchanged;
  the 2019–2025 tables have the digest K-03 was scored on
  (`42693a4a57591b37`); every window week is complete; the leak check
  passes at 2026 weeks 4, 5, 6 and 7; and the stored 2025 fold, re-run
  through the unchanged fold code on today's data, gives every number in
  `results/K-03.json`. One scored run. A second run is legitimate only to
  fix a bug, and the Result section would say why.
- Coverage seen before registering (no scores):
  - window: weeks 4–7 complete (16, 15, 14 and 14 games); week 8 has 12 of
    14 and is left out
  - kicker rows: 104 (weeks 4, 5, 6, 7: 29, 27, 24, 24)
  - `k_made_short`, `k_made_40`, `k_made_50`, `k_xp_made`, `k_missed`:
    100% every week
  - league rows by position (weeks 4/5/6/7): QB 32/32/28/31, RB
    87/80/73/71, WR 142/128/128/122, TE 76/80/68/67, K 29/27/24/24, DEF
    32/30/28/28; 768 matchups dealt
  - leak check: passed (2026 weeks 4, 5, 6 and 7)
  - training tables digest `42693a4a57591b37`, the one K-03 was scored on;
    2026 tables digest `235263ec7de3d2c7`
  - reproduction: the stored 2025 fold, re-run, gives every number in

## What a pass means
A pass makes K-03 a contender, nothing more. It still runs record-only for
two live weeks after this result is committed (ground rule 3) before any
serving decision. Nothing serves after the week-14 board (ground rule 8).
The page would keep its kicker mean and order; the distribution would feed
the ± and P(win). If the refitted normal on whole points does as well, the
cheaper change is the page's kicker sd, a separate decision (as `K-03.md`
says).

A fail means K-03 is not used as it stands. A redesign would be a new
pre-registration. Either way, K-03's DECISIONS.md row cites this result.

## Result

Run 2026-11-02T10:11:30+00:00 at `2406e9d` (one scored run;
`runs/K-03-2026.csv`). The harness (blob `af3ccb305c87`), this
pre-registration, K-03's module and the helpers are those of `39813d8`;
the two commits between changed other files. The helpers' combined blob
(`8bb29e1d71b7`) differs from the historical run's because `fb26c4b` fixed
three other helpers; `_p7_distributions.py` and `_common.py` are unchanged
since `6d588e2`. Before scoring, the run checked that the training tables
had K-03's digest, that weeks 4–7 were complete, that the leak check
passed at 2026 weeks 4–7 and that the stored 2025 fold reproduced exactly.
All four held.

The frozen fit on the 2,891 kicker rows of 2019–2025: κ = −1/12, as in
every historical fold (the next best on the grid were −1/10 and −1/15).
The fitted normal: a = 4.2294, b = 0.0332, so its sd is 4.44 to 4.56
points on the 2026 rows (4.49 on average), nearly flat as the review
found. The page's sd averages 4.62 on these rows.

**Fails.** The CRPS half holds; the P(win) half does not.
1. CRPS: 2.8695 against the page's 2.8884, a change of −0.0189 (0.65%
   lower). In history the change was −0.029 a fold on average (1.1%
   pooled). K-03 is lower in weeks 4, 6 and 7 and higher in week 5.
2. P(win): the pooled Brier is 0.242004 against the page's 0.241976, a
   change of +0.0000285. Not worse means ≤ on unrounded numbers, so this
   half fails. The week-clustered 95% interval is +0.0000065 to
   +0.0000505, and 1.1% of the bootstrap draws are not worse. With four
   weeks the interval is crude. K-03 is worse in weeks 4, 5 and 7 and
   level with the page in week 6 to five decimals.

Reported, never the bar:
- The review's cheaper alternative, the refitted normal on whole points,
  scores 2.8708. That is 93% of K-03's CRPS gain over the page (0.0176 of
  0.0189). K-03 is ahead of it by 0.0013 pooled: ahead in weeks 4 and 5,
  behind in weeks 6 and 7. The page's normal on whole points scores
  2.8835.
- For P(win) the fitted normal is worse than the page too: 0.242028, a
  change of +0.000052. The whole-point normals were not dealt into the
  leagues.
- Pinball loss at 10/50/90%: K-03 0.736 / 1.957 / 1.048, the page 0.763 /
  1.985 / 1.081, the fitted normal 0.747 / 1.985 / 1.076, the page on whole
  points 0.759 / 1.981 / 1.094, the refitted normal on whole points 0.756 /
  1.981 / 1.055. Log loss: K-03 0.677405, the page 0.677342, the fitted
  normal 0.677465.
- The 10–90% interval, read against each system's own share of it (with
  104 rows, one row moves a share by about one point): the page's normal
  holds 71.2% of the actuals against its 80%, and the fitted normal 73.1%
  against 80%. On whole points, where the closed interval takes in the
  mass at both ends: K-03 holds 78.8% against its own 83.9%, the page's
  normal 75.0% against 83.4%, and the refitted normal 76.9% against
  83.7%. Every system is short of its own share; K-03 is closest.

As pre-registered, K-03 is not used as it stands, and a redesign would be
a new pre-registration. On 2026 its CRPS gain over the page held but was
smaller, and nearly all of it comes from a refitted, nearly flat sd on
whole points. That alternative was reported here, not tested against a
bar: changing the page's kicker sd is a separate decision with its own
test. For P(win), neither K-03 nor the fitted normal beat the page on
these 768 matchups. One kicker a side moves a matchup little.
