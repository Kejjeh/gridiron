# TE-04: play-caller's two- and three-tight-end personnel rate, prior seasons (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen; the only runs
before this were `--coverage-only` (coverage and the leak check, no score).

## Idea
Roadmap TE-04, "Two- and three-tight-end usage by play-caller", from
participation personnel and the play-caller table (workstream B).
Participation ends with 2025 (nflverse has no 2026), so in-season personnel
is not available live and the habit can only enter as a prior: the
play-caller's career through the season before. The shipped TE set carries
the tight end's own snaps, target and air-yards share, expected points and
depth-chart rank this season; nothing about the personnel habit of the
person calling the plays, and no history from before this season.

## Hypothesis
A play-caller who lives in 12 and 13 personnel puts more tight ends on the
field and designs more of the offense around them, and he carries the habit
to a new team. That moves tight-end volume beyond what the tight end's
current snaps show, most when the season's sample is small or the
play-caller is new to the team. Volume (rule #6), as a slow prior.

## Features (`COLUMNS`)
| column | position | definition, as of the weeks before the row's week | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `pc_te2_rate` | TE | the week's play-caller (OPC) for the tight end's team: on his neutral plays in every season before this one in which the table names him play-caller (any team), the share with 2 or more tight ends in `offense_personnel`, minus the league's share in the same season: summed deviations ÷ (his plays + 200) | nflverse participation and play-by-play 2019–2024, the play-caller table |
| `pc_te3_rate` | TE | the same for 3 or more tight ends | same |

Neutral play (`_tendencies.py`): `play_type` pass or run (no-plays, spikes
and kneels never count), not a two-point try, not an aborted snap, 0.2 ≤ wp
≤ 0.8, quarters 1–3, not the last two minutes of the first half; joined to
participation on (`game_id`, `play_id`). Every neutral play of 2019–2025
has a personnel string (111,481 of 111,481). The tight ends are the count
before "TE" in the string. The league's 2+ TE share was 0.29–0.35 a season,
its 3+ TE share 0.035–0.056. The 200 plays of shrinkage were set before any
score (plays cluster within games, so more than a binomial count).

No part of this season enters (participation has no 2026), so the block
behaves the same in 2019–2025 as it would live.

Who is in charge: as in QB-03 (the table's OPC; no row means unknown).

Missing when: the team-week has no named play-caller, or he has no earlier
season as play-caller in the table, which is every 2019 row (the table
starts in 2019; 2016–2018 participation names no play-caller). Missing
enters as 0 plus `<col>__known`.
