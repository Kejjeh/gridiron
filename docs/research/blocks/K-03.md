# K-03: kicker points by distance tier, as a distribution (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen. The only runs
before this were `--coverage-only` (coverage and the leak check, no score)
and a plumbing test of the harness in which every real outcome was replaced
by a random draw before anything was fitted, so no real score exists.

## Idea
Roadmap K-03, "Expected points per attempt by distance, league tiers" (kind
D, workstream D). The page gives a kicker one normal: the served mean (the
advanced K ridge, `K_ADV`) and the baseline's sd, max(0.55 × baseline, 1.5)
(`gridiron.projection`; the page swaps in the advanced mean and keeps this
sd). This league scores field goals by distance (3 inside 40, 4 from 40–49,
5 from 50+), 1 per extra point and −1 per miss (`KICKING_SCORING`), so a
kicker's week is a sum of a few lumpy events on whole points. The shipped
kicker ridge already carries the mean (team attempts per game, implied
total); a tier expectation used as a MEAN would be a feature block. Here the
tiers shape the distribution around the page's own mean.

## Hypothesis
Kicker points are a small count of discrete events, and the tier mix (a team
that kicks long, a kicker who misses) sets their spread and lumps. A count
model on whole points, with the right dispersion, should describe a
kicker's week better than one normal. Attempts are volume (the team's,
season to date, fast); make rates are efficiency (heavily shrunk, slow:
rule #6).

## Distribution inputs (`COLUMNS`)
| column | position | definition, as of the weeks before the row's week | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `k_made_short` | K | expected made field goals inside 40 per game: λ(0–29) · p(0–29) + λ(30–39) · p(30–39) | team and player box scores |
| `k_made_40` | K | λ(40–49) · p(40–49) | same |
| `k_made_50` | K | λ(50+) · p(50+) | same |
| `k_xp_made` | K | λ(XP) · p(XP) | same |
| `k_missed` | K | Σ over the four tiers of λ · (1 − p), plus λ(XP) · (1 − p(XP)) | same |

λ(t) = (his team's attempts in tier t this season + 4 · L(t)) / (team-games
+ 4), attempts = made + missed, L(t) = last season's league attempts per
team-game in t (nflverse `load_team_stats`, `fg_made_*` / `fg_missed_*`,
`pat_made` / `pat_missed`). p(t) = (his makes in t this season + 20 · P(t)) /
(his attempts in t + 20), P(t) = last season's league make rate in t; 40 in
place of 20 for extra points (player box scores, by gsis id; the kicking
rows are the player-games with a kick in them, read from the box score, not
from the roster position tag: rule #4). A blocked kick
scores 0 in this league's formula (`kicker_points` reads `fg_missed`, which
leaves blocks out) and is left out. Five columns, one per distinct point
value in `KICKING_SCORING`.

Missing when: never (a team or kicker with no earlier game gets the league
prior).

## Distribution (`pmf`, `fit`; shared code `blocks/_p7_distributions.py`)
For a row with the page's mean μ:
- points = 3·N_short + 4·N_40 + 5·N_50 + 1·N_xp − 1·N_miss, the values read
  from `KICKING_SCORING` (asserted: one value inside 40, one miss value);
- each count's mean is c × its input, one c per row so the mean is exactly
  μ (c = μ / Σ value × input): a higher page mean means more kicks, with
  the tier mix kept;
- the counts' dispersion κ: one value per fold, fitted on the six training
  seasons by minimum mean CRPS over the grid {−1/10, −1/12, −1/15, −1/20,
  −1/30, −1/50, 0, 0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.35, 0.5}. κ < 0 is a multinomial over
  −1/κ trials (scoring events crowd each other out: a drive ends one way),
  κ = 0 independent Poisson counts, κ > 0 a shared gamma factor (a big day
  for the offense lifts every count). A multinomial row whose scaled events
  reach 95% of its trials uses Poisson;
- exact on the integer lattice from the characteristic function (FFT,
  −20 to 107 points); what the FFT window leaves out (about 1e-12 of the
  mass) is put back on whole points by `on_grid_mean`, so the mean is
  exactly μ and every point a whole number (the harness refuses a run where
  any row's mean differs from μ by more than 1e-6).
- μ is the harness's: the K ridge (`K_ADV`) refit with the row's season held
  out, floored at 0; the training rows' μ come from models fit on the five
  seasons other than the test season and their own.

## Test
- Position: K.
- Harness: `scripts/research/distribution_backtest.py` (block_runner.py's
  tables, rows and folds; pwin_calibration.py's leagues; neither changed).
- Rows: block_runner.py's kicker table (k_def_backtest.py's, weeks 4–18,
  Sleeper projected, actual known), 2019–2025, seven season folds. The 2019
  table has no Raiders rows (31 teams), by design.
- Comparator: the page's normal, N(μ, max(0.55 × baseline, 1.5)).
- Bar: a lower mean CRPS than the page's normal in every one of the seven
  folds, AND the pooled P(win) Brier over the seven folds' synthetic leagues
  not worse (≤), both decided on unrounded numbers. Leagues:
  pwin_calibration.py's 12-team leagues (its dealing,
  seeds and slot pools, checked against its own `week_matchups`), dealt from
  the fold's rows and means, weeks 4–17, 32 seeded leagues a week. Every
  starter keeps the page's normal except the kickers, whose distributions
  are this one; P(win) is computed exactly on the lattice.
- Reported, never the bar: pinball loss at 10/50/90%, the share of actuals
  inside the 10–90% interval, log loss, a week-clustered bootstrap of the
  Brier change; a fitted normal N(μ, a + bμ) fitted the same way on the same
  rows (is a gain the shape, or only a refitted spread?); and the page's
  normal put on whole points (how much of a gain is only knowing that kicker
  points are whole numbers?).
- Coverage seen before registering (no scores):
  - `k_made_short`, `k_made_40`, `k_made_50`, `k_xp_made`, `k_missed`: 100%
    every season
  - leak check: passed (2021 week 9, 2024 week 14)

**PASS** on the pre-registered bar. CRPS is lower than the page's normal
in all 7 folds, by 0.012 to 0.048 points (mean -0.029; pooled 2.521
against 2.549). The pooled P(win) Brier is not worse: 0.238118 against
0.238118 over 18,811 matchups, an unrounded change of -0.0000002
(week-clustered 95% interval -0.0000086 to +0.0000085). That half is a
tie, not a gain: one kicker a side barely moves a matchup.

K-03 is below both pre-registered references in every fold. Pooled
pinball loss at 10/50/90% is 0.701 / 1.788 / 0.860 against the page's
0.717 / 1.799 / 0.882 (the median is worse in 2024 alone). The fitted
dispersion was κ = -1/12 in every fold, inside the grid: a multinomial
over 12 trials, about one kicking chance a drive.

Corrected 2026-11-02 (review): most of the CRPS gain is not the shape.
About 78% of it comes from a refitted, nearly flat kicker sd plus whole
points; the count model adds the rest. Each pre-registered reference
tests only half of that. The fitted normal refits the sd but stays
continuous. The whole-point normal keeps the page's sd. A review re-run
(scratch scripts, not committed) did both at once: a normal around the
page's mean, on whole points, with sd = a + b·μ, (a, b) fitted by
minimum CRPS on the same training rows as the fitted normal. The re-run
reproduces every CRPS in the table. The refitted whole-point normal's
pooled CRPS is 2.5271, against K-03's 2.5209 and the page's 2.5494:
0.0223 of K-03's pooled gain of 0.0285. It beats K-03 in 2019 (2.3513

points on average a fold. The page's sd, max(0.55 × baseline, 1.5),
