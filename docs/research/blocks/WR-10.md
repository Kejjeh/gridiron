# WR-10: rookie and early-season priors, draft capital and the 40 for rookies, last season for veterans (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen. The only runs
before this were `--coverage-only` (coverage, feature counts and the leak
check, no score) and a dry run of the scoring code on random synthetic
outcomes (no real points).

## Idea
Roadmap WR-10, "Rookie and early-season priors" (draft picks, combine,
prior stats; kind A, phase 4, for 2027). It is tested now on 2019–2025, so
the offseason only re-tests it on 2026.

What the page does in weeks 1–3 today: the advanced model and the stack
start in week 4 (`min_week`). Before that the page serves
`gridiron.projection` on this season's box scores alone. In week 1 it
abstains for every player. In weeks 2–3 it projects a player who has an
earlier game from one or two games of usage, shrunk to role priors, and
abstains on the rest. A rookie gets no number until he has played, and the
page never reads the draft, the combine or last season. The shipped set
(expected points, shares, depth chart) does not run in these weeks, so the
only overlap is the page's own in-season number, and every system below
carries it.

This is not a runner block: `block_runner.py` scores weeks 4–18. Its
harness is `scripts/research/early_season_backtest.py`, and the script's
docstring holds the shared method.

## Hypothesis
A rookie receiver has no last season. Teams play the receivers they drafted
high, so draft capital says how much a rookie will be used from week 1. His
40-yard time adds a little about the role he was drafted for (speed
outside). A veteran keeps last season's points per game, discounted; that
plain prior is shared with QB-12 and TE-13. All of these are facts fixed
before week 1. The 40 is a physical trait, so it is a slow prior (rule #6).
The bar does not test signs; the per-unit coefficients of the frozen fit are
reported.

## Features
| column | position | definition, as of before the row's week | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `page` | WR | the page's number today: `gridiron.projection.project` on this season's box scores before the week, with the week's implied total; missing where it abstains | box scores, schedules (lines) |
| `ppg_prev` | WR | his league points per game over last season's regular-season games (any team); missing with fewer than 4 games | box scores |
| `rookie_capital` | WR | for a rookie (the players table's `rookie_season` is this season): −ln(overall pick), with an undrafted rookie at pick 260; missing for everyone else | nflverse draft picks, players table |
| `rookie_forty` | WR | for a rookie: his combine 40-yard time; missing for everyone else, and for a rookie with no recorded 40 | nflverse combine, joined through the crosswalk's PFR id |

Missing when: `page` in week 1, and for a player with no earlier game this
season; `ppg_prev` with fewer than 4 games last season; both rookie columns
for every non-rookie, and `rookie_forty` for a rookie with no 40. Missing
enters as 0 plus `<col>__known`, so `rookie_capital__known` is the rookie
flag.

## Test
- Position: WR. Rows: every week 1–3 player-week of 2019–2025 with a box
  score and a Sleeper projection: 3,024 rows, 1,708 of them with Sleeper 5+.
- Systems, each fitted separately for weeks 1, 2 and 3 on the training
  seasons (`A.Ridge`, lam 5), with fitted predictions floored at 0:
  - `null`: the training mean.
  - `today`: the page as served, or the null where it abstains.
  - `today_fit`: [page].
  - `prior`: [page, ppg_prev], last season discounted.
  - `cand`: prior plus [rookie_capital, rookie_forty].

  Sleeper's projection is context only, never part of the bar.
- Seasons: 2019–2025, seven season folds; every other season trains.
- Bar (all three must hold):
  1. `cand` beats both `today` and `today_fit` on MAE and on start/sit
     pairwise (within a week, among Sleeper 5+) in every fold. This must
     hold over weeks 1–3 and again over weeks 2–3 alone.
  2. `cand` beats `prior` on MAE and pairwise in every fold (weeks 1–3):
     the draft and the combine add something.
  3. Out-of-fold `cand`, 2019–2025 pooled: |bias| ≤ 1.5 points in every band
     with n ≥ 10. The bands are last season's points per game (none, <5,
     5–10, 10–15, 15–20, 20+) and rookies by draft round (R1, R2, R3–4, R5–7,
     undrafted), with veterans as one band.

  `prior` is judged on conditions 1 and 3 as well, and reported as
  `prior_clears`. That result is WR-10's veteran clause on its own; it is
  not a pass for WR-10.

## If it passes
The frozen fit (`frozen_fit` in the JSON, fitted on all seven seasons) is
applied unchanged to 2026 weeks 1–3 in the offseason (phase 4). Nothing has
looked at 2026 weeks 1–3 with it. Only if it holds there does it get a
DECISIONS.md row and a place on the 2027 week 1–3 board. Nothing changes in
2026.

fit is re-tested unchanged on 2026 weeks 1–3 in the offseason, and only then
