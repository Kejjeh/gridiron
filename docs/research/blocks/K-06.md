# K-06: the coach's trust in long field goals, and the kicker's accuracy by distance (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

## Idea
Roadmap K-06, "Coach's trust in long range, and accuracy by distance" (the
roadmap's note: accuracy is a slow prior), from play-by-play. In this
league a make from 50+ is worth 5, from 40-49 4, from under 40 3, and a miss
costs 1. The shipped kicker set counts his team's field-goal attempts per
game (`team_fga_pg`) but not how long they are, and nothing in it measures
his accuracy: his points per game (`baseline`, `k_ppg_season`, `k_ppg_l3`)
mix distance, accuracy and volume.

## Hypothesis
A kicker whose coach sends him out from 50+ more often than the league
scores more per attempt, and a kicker who makes more than the league at the
same distances misses less. The long share is the coach's decision
(volume-like) but rests on about 31 attempts a season, so it is shrunk
(126 attempts) and last season enters as its own column; accuracy is
efficiency, so it is a slow prior over his last three seasons and this one,
shrunk with 82 attempts (rule #6).

## Features (`COLUMNS`)
| column | position(s) | definition, as of the weeks before the row's week | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `fg50_share_oe` | K | his team's field-goal attempts from 50+ yards (`kick_distance`), minus its attempts times the league's 50+ share to date, over its attempts plus 126; its games this season before the row's week | nflverse play-by-play (play_type field_goal) |
| `fg50_share_prior_oe` | K | the same over his team's whole previous season, against that season's league share | same |
| `k_fg_oe` | K | his field goals made minus expected, over his attempts plus 82: expected is the league's make rate at the attempt's 5-yard distance bucket (15-19, 20-24, ..., 60+) over the three seasons before the row's season; his attempts (for any team) in those three seasons and this season before the row's week; blocked counts as a miss | play-by-play (`kicker_player_id`, `field_goal_result`) |

method of moments (QUANT_FOUNDATIONS §5.1) on the 2012-2018 team- and
kicker-seasons (kickers with 10+ attempts), seasons no fold scores, from the
play-by-play alone.
