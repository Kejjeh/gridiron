# QB-10: the opponent's man/zone mix against the QB's own man/zone split (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen; the only runs
before this were `--coverage-only` (coverage and the leak check, no score).
The other checks looked at the features' own distributions and at how much
of a QB's split is signal rather than sampling noise; none read points.

## Idea
Roadmap QB-10, "Opponent man/zone vs the QB's splits", from participation
coverage (to 2025). The roadmap's note: prior only; efficiency, so slow
(rule #6). The shipped QB set carries his overall efficiency (`fpoe_season`,
`ngs_cpoe`) and the opponent's overall QB points allowed (`def_allowed`).
Neither knows the opponent's coverage mix, or how this QB does against each
kind of coverage. No FEATURE_EXPANSION block touched coverage.

## Hypothesis
Some QBs are better against man than against zone, beyond the league's own
gap. Such a QB should gain against an opponent that played more man than the
league last season, and lose against a zone-heavy one. This is efficiency,
so it enters only as a prior-season split, shrunk hard toward the league's.

## Features (`COLUMNS`)
| column | position | definition, as of the weeks before the row's week | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `man_split_prior` | QB | his EPA per dropback against man minus against zone, minus the league's man-minus-zone gap; season s−1, his regular-season dropbacks as the play's passer (`passer_id`: scrambles and sacks included) where participation records man or zone. Empirical Bayes: the gap times h ÷ (h + 900), with h = n_man·n_zone ÷ (n_man + n_zone) the split's effective number of dropbacks (0 when he has none against one of the two) | participation `defense_man_zone_type` × play-by-play `epa` |
| `opp_man_rate_prior` | QB | the opponent defense's share of its known-coverage dropbacks faced in s−1 that were man, minus the league's share that season | same |
| `man_matchup_prior` | QB | `man_split_prior × opp_man_rate_prior`: his expected EPA-per-dropback edge from this opponent's mix | derived |

**The split is mostly noise, and the shrinkage says so.** Measured on the
inputs alone (method of moments, QB-seasons with 100+ known-coverage
dropbacks and 20+ against each side): the observed spread of QBs' centered
splits is 0.0437 (variance), of which sampling noise is 0.0410. That leaves
τ ≈ 0.05 EPA per dropback over 2018–2025, and no detectable spread at all
over 2018–2024 alone. The same QB's split correlates 0.11 from one season to
the next (220 pairs). k = σ² ÷ τ² = 2.42 ÷ 0.0026 ≈ 900. Other measures
were checked the same way and are worse: EPA capped at ±3, success rate,
yards per dropback and CPOE have year-to-year correlations of 0.09, −0.02,
0.02 and −0.10. So the block tests EPA, the roadmap's measure. A regular
starter's split keeps about a tenth of its raw size.

Missing when: he had no dropback with man or zone recorded in s−1 (a rookie,
or no snaps last season). That makes `man_split_prior` and
`man_matchup_prior` missing. Missing enters as 0 plus `<col>__known`, and
that indicator is close to "he passed in the NFL last season", which may
carry signal of its own. `opp_man_rate_prior` is never missing.

Real share: participation records man or zone on 88.6% (2018), 88.9%
(2019), 89.3% (2020), 89.0% (2021), 88.6% (2022), 99.6% (2023) and 99.8%
(2024) of regular-season dropbacks, the seasons the priors read. The splits
and the man rates rest on those plays. The league's man share moves with the
source: 35.0%, 37.2%, 34.4%, 30.3%, 28.8% (2018–2022), then 41.3% (2023),
48.5% (2024) and 31.0% (2025). So every rate here is taken relative to its
own season's league value.

Shared builders: `scripts/research/blocks/_p4_participation_priors.py`
(package P4): participation joined to regular-season play-by-play on (game,
play), so participation's playoff plays never enter.
