# K-11: the opponent's "bend but don't break", drives to its 30 that end without a touchdown (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen; the only runs
before this were `--coverage-only` (coverage and the leak check, no score)
and feature-only summaries (counts, distributions). No actual points were
read.

## Idea
Roadmap K-11, "Opponent 'bend but don't break'", from play-by-play: the
opponent defense's drives allowed that reached its 30 or closer and ended
without the offense's touchdown (a field goal, a miss, a turnover, downs,
the end of a half), per game. The shipped kicker set has nothing on the
opponent's defense but the line (`implied`, `spread`), and QUANT_FOUNDATIONS
§1.3 found that field goals do not scale with the implied total: the line
prices the points a defense allows, not whether they come as touchdowns or
kicks.

## Hypothesis
A defense that lets drives into field-goal range but keeps them out of the
end zone gives the kicker facing it more attempts than the line and his own
team's rates say. These are opportunities for the kicker (volume), so they
update fast (rule #6): season to date and the opponent's last three games,
as the shipped set reads his own points.
