# WR-09: screen targets per game and share of the team's screens (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen; the only runs
before this were `--coverage-only` (coverage and the leak check, no score).

## Idea
Roadmap WR-09, "Screens and designed touches", from FTN charting's
`is_screen_pass` (the free nflverse subset, 2022 on, live in 2026). The
shipped WR set carries target share and air-yards share (last 3), expected
points (which price a screen's short air yards), Next Gen Stats separation
and YAC over expected, and points over expected. A screen-heavy receiver
shows up only as a high target share next to a low air-yards share; nothing
in the set says the targets were designed for him.

## Hypothesis
A screen is a designed touch: the play is called for him, so the volume is
sticky (the staff keeps calling it) and catchable (half a point a reception
in this league). A receiver with more screen targets per game, or a larger
share of his team's screens, keeps that volume, and catches more than
expected points priced from air yards say. Volume (rule #6), season to
date.

## Features (`COLUMNS`)
| column | position(s) | definition, as of the weeks before the row's week | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `screen_tgt_pg` | WR | his screen targets (FTN `is_screen_pass` on a play-by-play pass attempt, `play_type == "pass"`, with him as the receiver; two-point tries and no-plays excluded) per FTN-charted game he played this season | FTN charting joined to play-by-play on (`game_id`, `play_id`); player box scores for his games |
| `screen_tgt_share` | WR | his screen targets ÷ his teams' screen targets, over the charted games he played this season | same |

games was charted, which is every row of 2019-2021 (FTN starts in 2022);
the share also when his teams threw no screen in those games (20 rows).
