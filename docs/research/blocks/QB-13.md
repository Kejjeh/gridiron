# QB-13: rest days, Thursday games, and zones crossed eastward for an early kickoff (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen; the only runs
before this were `--coverage-only` (coverage and the leak check, no score).

## Idea
Roadmap QB-13, "Short-week and west-to-east travel splits", from the
schedules. The shipped QB set has nothing from the calendar or from travel:
its game context is the team's implied total (`implied`) and the opponent's
points allowed to quarterbacks (`def_allowed`). The closing line can price a
short week or a long trip only through the team's total, the same for every
player on the team.

**What it can measure:** whether a quarterback scores differently on a short
week, after extra rest, or after his team crossed time zones eastward for a
1 p.m. Eastern (or earlier) kickoff, beyond what the shipped set says.

**What it cannot measure:** when a team actually travelled (a London trip
often leaves days early), sleep, or the opponent's rest (DEF-10 has that).
Thursday games are few (about 7% of rows), so the flag rests on a small
sample per season.

## Hypothesis
A short week leaves less time to prepare and recover, and a team that crossed
time zones eastward for an early kickoff plays in its body clock's morning;
both should lower a quarterback's output a little, and extra rest (after a
bye) should raise it. These are facts of the calendar, known before the
week; nothing here is efficiency (rule #6).

## Features (`COLUMNS`)
| column | position | definition, as of the weeks before the row's week | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `rest_days` | QB | days since his team's previous game (the schedule's `home_rest` or `away_rest` for his side), clipped to [4, 14] | nflverse pre-game schedule |
| `thursday` | QB | 1 when his team's game is on a Thursday, else 0 | same |
| `east_early_tz` | QB | when the kickoff is at or before 13:00 Eastern: the time zones his team crossed eastward to the venue (the venue's UTC offset − his team's home UTC offset at kickoff, DST-aware; 0 when westward or none; capped at 3); 0 for a later kickoff | schedule (kickoff, stadium), `data/reference/team_timezones.csv`, `data/reference/stadiums.csv` |

Definitions (`_p5_schedule_weather.py`): his team is the team of his box
score that week (the runner's keys). A team's home zone is the IANA zone of
the stadiums of its home games, 2019–2026 (`team_timezones.csv`, a source on
every row). The venue is the schedule's `stadium_id`, except a neutral-site
game listed at the home team's own stadium (nflverse lists the six 2025
international games from week 4 on that way): that venue is unknown. The
kickoff is the schedule's `gameday` and `gametime`, Eastern clock time.

Missing when: the venue is unknown (`east_early_tz` only: those six 2025
games). Missing enters as 0 plus `<col>__known`.

Distributions seen before registering (no scores): `rest_days` median 7,
95th percentile 14; `thursday` is 1 on 6.6% of rows; `east_early_tz` is
nonzero on 10.4% of rows (mean 0.20, maximum 3).
