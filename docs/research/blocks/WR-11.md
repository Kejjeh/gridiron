# WR-11: forecast wind above 10 mph, and its product with his deep-target share (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen; the only runs
before this were `--coverage-only` (coverage and the leak check, no score).

## Idea
Roadmap WR-11, "Deep threats in wind", from Open-Meteo and depth of target
(low priority). The roadmap names Next Gen Stats aDOT; this block reads the
depth of every target from play-by-play instead, which covers every
receiver (Next Gen Stats rows exist only above a volume threshold; in 2025,
depth of target for 37% of rows, FEATURE_EXPANSION.md). Weather failed in
general (the `+line` block, 4 of 7 folds). The shipped WR set has his
air-yards share (`ay_share_l3`, his share of the team's air yards: volume),
target share, expected points and the team's implied total (the market
lowers totals for wind), and nothing on the weather.

**What it can measure:** whether wind at kickoff costs a receiver whose
targets are deep more than one whose targets are short, beyond the implied
total.

**What it cannot measure:** the forecast a lineup decision sees days before
kickoff (Open-Meteo's archived forecast is assembled from the first hours of
each model run, close to the observation), gusts, the wind's direction to
the field, or the wind inside a stadium bowl. His quarterback's response to
the wind is in the outcome, not in the feature.

## Hypothesis
Wind above about 10 mph cuts and scatters long passes most, so a receiver
who lives on targets of 20+ air yards should lose more in wind than a
receiver of short targets, who may even gain some. The wind is a pre-game
fact of this week's game; the deep share is the kind of target he gets (a
volume split, season to date, shrunk toward the league's rate; rule #6).

## Features (`COLUMNS`)
| column | position | definition, as of the weeks before the row's week | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `wind10` | WR | the venue's wind at kickoff: the mean of the hourly 10 m wind speed at the kickoff hour and the two hours after it (mph), minus 10, floored at 0; 0 indoors (roof dome or closed) | Open-Meteo historical forecast (`gfs_seamless`), ERA5 reanalysis where the forecast has no values; nflverse pre-game schedule (kickoff, roof, stadium); `data/reference/stadiums.csv` |
| `wind10_x_deep` | WR | `wind10` × (his deep share − the league's rate): his deep share is (his targets with `air_yards` ≥ 20 + 10 × the league's rate) ÷ (his targets with air yards + 10), over his games this season; the league's rate is the share of all such targets of the previous season with 20+ air yards (every receiver; no position tag, rule #4) | the above; nflverse play-by-play, box scores |

Definitions (`_p5_schedule_weather.py`): a target is the P1a definition
(`_p1a_pbp_roles.py`: play_type `pass`, `pass_attempt` 1, a receiver named,
not a two-point try) with air yards recorded. The kickoff hour is the
schedule's `gameday` and `gametime` (Eastern) in UTC, floored to the hour.
The venue is the schedule's `stadium_id`, except a neutral-site game listed
at the home team's own stadium (the six 2025 international games from week 4
on), whose venue and weather are unknown. The 10 mph floor and the 20-yard
line are fixed before any score.

Weather data by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0). Weeks 4–18 of 2019–2025 (1,535
games): 1,045 from the archived forecast, 5 from the observed reanalysis
(the five 2019 international games, before the forecast archive covers
Europe and Mexico), 479 indoors (0 and 0), and 6 unknown (the 2025
international games listed at a home stadium); no outdoor game is left
without a value. Over the 1,050 outdoor games the forecast wind is above 10
mph in 22.6% (above 15 mph in 5.1%) and the precipitation 1 mm or more in
6.4%. Checked against the schedule's observed wind (955 outdoor games where
it has one): correlation 0.73, means 7.4 and 8.6 mph.

Missing when: the venue is unknown (`wind10` and the product), or he has no
earlier target this season while `wind10` > 0 (the product; 0 when `wind10`
is 0). Missing enters as 0 plus `<col>__known`.

Distributions seen before registering (no scores): `wind10` is nonzero on
15.3% of WR rows (mean 0.52, 95th percentile 4.2, maximum 20.2);
`wind10_x_deep` on 14.6% (SD 0.136, range −1.17 to 3.60). His deep share:
mean 0.151, SD 0.069; the league's rate is 0.114–0.123 across 2018–2024.
