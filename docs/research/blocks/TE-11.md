# TE-11: the opponent's targets allowed to tight ends, per game and as a share (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen; the only runs
before this were `--coverage-only` (coverage and the leak check, no score).

## Idea
Roadmap TE-11, "Opponent coverage of tight ends" (the roadmap's note: weak
signal, cheap test). The shipped `def_allowed` already holds the fantasy
points the opponent allowed to tight ends per game this season, minus the
league. This tests the volume side instead: how many targets the opponent
lets tight ends draw.

## Hypothesis
Points allowed to tight ends are driven by a handful of touchdowns, so they
are noisy. Targets allowed are many more events, and say where a defense's
coverage sends the ball. A tight end facing a defense that gives tight ends
more targets, per game or as a share of all the targets it allows, gets more
volume than `def_allowed` alone predicts. Volume, season to date, relative
to the league over the same weeks.

## Features (`COLUMNS`)
| column | position(s) | definition, as of the weeks before the row's week | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `opp_te_tgt_pg_rel` | TE | targets to tight ends (box-score position TE) in the opponent's games this season, per game, minus the league's per team-game figure over the same weeks | nflverse player box scores (`targets`, `position`, `opponent_team`) |
| `opp_te_tgt_share_rel` | TE | tight ends' share of all the targets the opponent allowed this season, minus the league's share over the same weeks | same |

The share separates "faces many passes" (which the implied total and game
script already carry) from "funnels them to tight ends".
