## Idea
Roadmap K-08, "Kicker changes and call-ups" (kind A), cast as features on
the kicker rows of `k_def_backtest.py`'s table. The shipped K features
(`baseline`, `k_ppg_season`, `k_ppg_l3`, `team_fga_pg`, `team_xpa_pg`,
`implied`, `spread`, `dome`) are computed per kicker from his own games this
season: a kicker in his first game of the season has every one of them
missing, the team's included, and a kicker who changed teams carries his
old team's production. Nothing in the set says that he is new with this
team, its snapper and its holder, or that last week someone else kicked.
Tenure comes from box scores (who kicked for whom, rule #4), not from the
roster or Sleeper's player map (2026 only).

## Hypothesis
A kicker new to the team, signed or called up after an injury or a release,
scores less than the shipped features say: a replacement kicker is
likely to be less accurate, and a coach tries fewer long field goals with
one he does not trust yet; the effect fades with games together. Accuracy
enters only through tenure, a slow structural fact, never through his recent
make rate (rule #6).

## Features (`COLUMNS`)
| column | position(s) | definition, as of the weeks before the row's week | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `k_new_team` | K | 1 when he has no earlier regular-season game with a field-goal or extra-point attempt for this team, in the box scores from four seasons before the row's season on | player box scores (fg_att, pat_att) |
| `k_team_games` | K | his regular-season games with a field-goal or extra-point attempt for this team before this week, capped at 16 | same |
| `k_team_changed` | K | 1 when the team's kicker in its previous game with a kick (the player with the most field-goal plus extra-point attempts for it) was someone else | same |

Missing when: the team has no earlier kicking game in the box scores read
(never on these rows). It would enter as 0 plus `__known`.

Shared code: `scripts/research/blocks/_p2b_events_ramps_defense.py` (`box`,
a lean box-score loader cut like `weekly`). Four seasons of history (2015
on for the 2019 fold) are enough for the cap: every fold sees the same
depth.

On the 2,891 K rows of 2019-2025: `k_new_team` is 1 on 23 rows (2019 2,
2020 2, 2021 6, 2022 1, 2023 none, 2024 6, 2025 6), `k_team_changed` on 74
rows (3-16 a season), and `k_team_games` is under the cap on 965 rows. The
change rows are few: of the 98 first games with a team in the box scores
(weeks 4 on, 2019-2025), 37 are in the kicker table and 23 of those have a
Sleeper number, the table's condition. The test has little power.
