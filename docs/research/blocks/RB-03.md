# RB-03: share of the team's carries inside the opponent's 5 (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen; the only run
before this was `--coverage-only` (coverage and the leak check, no score).

## Idea
Roadmap RB-03, "Share of carries inside the 5", from play-by-play. The
roadmap's note: expected touchdowns failed overall (`+xtd`,
FEATURE_EXPANSION.md); this tests the role instead. The shipped set already
carries expected fantasy points (`xfp_l3`, `xfp_season`, which price every
carry by its field position), carries (`carries_l3`), opportunities and the
depth-chart rank.

## Hypothesis
The goal-line role is sticky within a season and expected points average it
away: a back who gets most of his team's carries inside the 5 scores more
touchdowns than his expected points alone say. This is volume (a share of
opportunities), so it updates fast (rule #6).

## Features (`COLUMNS`)
| column | position | definition, as of the weeks before the row's week | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `gl_share_std` | RB | his rushing attempts with `yardline_100 <= 5` (two-point tries excluded) ÷ his teams' such attempts, over the games he played this season | nflverse play-by-play, player box scores |
| `gl_share_l3` | RB | the same over his last 3 games | same |
| `gl_carries_pg` | RB | his carries inside the 5 per game this season | same |

Missing when: no earlier game this season (all three), or his teams had no
carries inside the 5 in those games (the shares). Missing enters as 0 plus
`<col>__known`.
