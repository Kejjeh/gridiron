# WR-04: red-zone and end-zone target share, wide receivers (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen; the only runs
before this were `--coverage-only` (coverage and the leak check, no score).

## Idea
Roadmap WR-04, "End-zone and red-zone target share", from play-by-play. The
shipped WR set already carries his overall target share (`tgt_share_l3`), his
air-yards share (`ay_share_l3`) and expected points (`xfp_l3`, `xfp_season`,
`xfp_last`). Expected points price every past target by its air yards and
field position, so a receiver who drew red-zone targets already shows higher
expected points. RB-03 (the goal-line carry share) failed in 0 of 7 folds,
read as expected points already carrying that role. What this block adds is
the role in the share form: his part of
the team's scoring-area targets, normalised for how often the team gets there.
A deep target's air yards and a 5-yard end-zone target look nothing alike in
`ay_share_l3`.

## Hypothesis
Who gets the targets in the red zone and the end zone is a sticky role
within a season. A receiver who holds that role scores more touchdowns than
his overall target share and his expected points say. This is volume (a share
of opportunities), so it updates fast (rule #6): season to date and last 3.

## Features (`COLUMNS`)
| column | position | definition, as of the weeks before the row's week | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `rz_tgt_share_std` | WR | his targets with `yardline_100` ≤ 20 ÷ his teams' such targets, over his games this season | nflverse play-by-play, snap counts, box scores |
| `rz_tgt_share_l3` | WR | the same over his last 3 games | same |
| `ez_tgt_share_std` | WR | his end-zone targets (`air_yards` ≥ `yardline_100`: the throw reaches the goal line, from anywhere on the field) ÷ his teams' end-zone targets, over his games this season | same |
| `ez_tgt_share_l3` | WR | the same over his last 3 games | same |

Definitions (`_p1a_pbp_roles.py`, shared with TE-03): a target is play_type
`pass`, `pass_attempt` 1, a receiver named, not a two-point try. It matches
the box-score targets exactly. His games are the games he was on the field
for on offense: offensive snaps > 0 in the snap counts, or a box score with a
carry, target or pass attempt. Box scores alone would drop the 10% of the
target receivers' games that had no stat line, and those games' team targets
with them.

Missing when: no earlier game this season (all four), or his teams had no
such targets in those games (the share). Missing enters as 0 plus
`<col>__known`.
