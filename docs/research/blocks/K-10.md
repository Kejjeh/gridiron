## Idea
Roadmap K-10, "End-of-half clock management", from play-by-play: his team's
field-goal attempts snapped with two minutes or less left in the first or
second half. The shipped kicker set counts all of his team's attempts per
game (`team_fga_pg`, season to date) and has the line; it cannot tell a
team whose attempts come from a two-minute drill that works from one whose
attempts come from stalled drives.

## Hypothesis
Ending a half with a field goal is a play-calling and clock habit (a
quarterback and coach who move the ball late and stop the clock in range)
that holds across games and seasons, so a team that kicks more at the end
of halves gives its kicker more attempts than its overall rate and the line
say. Volume, so it enters raw and fast (rule #6): season to date, plus the
previous season for a steadier read.
