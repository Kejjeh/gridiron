# K-09: extra point or two, the team's two-point tendency after touchdowns (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen; the only runs
before this were `--coverage-only` (coverage and the leak check, no score)
and feature-only summaries (counts, distributions, the prior strengths on
2012-2018). No actual points were read.

## Idea
Roadmap K-09, "Extra point vs two-point tendency", from play-by-play. A
two-point try takes an extra point (1 point) away from the kicker. The
shipped kicker set has his team's extra-point attempts per game
(`team_xpa_pg`, season to date) and the implied total: together they mix
how often the team scores touchdowns with how often it kicks after them,
over a few games. Nothing in it says the coach goes for two by habit.

## Hypothesis
A team that goes for two more often than the league, a coach's habit that
carries from game to game and season to season, gives its kicker fewer
extra points per touchdown than `team_xpa_pg` and the line say. The choice
is volume (rule #6), but it rests on about 40 tries a season, so the season
to date is shrunk (49 tries, about one season) and last season enters as
its own column.

## Features (`COLUMNS`)
| column | position(s) | definition, as of the weeks before the row's week | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `two_pt_oe` | K | his team's two-point tries per conversion try (extra-point kicks plus two-point tries; a try wiped out by a penalty is not one), over the league's rate to date, shrunk: (two − tries·m)/(tries + 49); its games this season before the row's week | nflverse play-by-play (play_type extra_point, `two_point_attempt` on pass or run plays) |
| `two_pt_prior_oe` | K | the same over his team's whole previous season, against that season's league rate | same |

The league's two-point rate per conversion try, 2019-2025: 0.083, 0.089,
0.109, 0.090, 0.094, 0.098, 0.092 (110 to 153 tries a season). Both
columns are measured against the league of the same period, so a
league-wide shift moves no team. The prior strength is the method of
moments (QUANT_FOUNDATIONS §5.1) on the 2012-2018 team-seasons, seasons no
fold scores, from the play-by-play alone.

Missing when: never on these rows (a team with no try yet would be 0, the
league). No `__known` indicator.

Shared code: `scripts/research/blocks/_p6_kicker_defense_pbp.py`
(`per_team_game`, `league_as_of`, `over_league`). On the 2,891 kicker rows
the within-season SD is 0.021-0.033 (`two_pt_oe`) and 0.023-0.037
(`two_pt_prior_oe`).

extra-point attempts per game already carry most of that. Not shipped, not
