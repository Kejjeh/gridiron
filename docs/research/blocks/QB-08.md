# QB-08: play-caller's red-zone pass rate over expected (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen; the only runs
before this were `--coverage-only` (coverage and the leak check, no score).

## Idea
Roadmap QB-08, "Red-zone pass rate over expected by play-caller", from
play-by-play `pass_oe` inside the opponent's 20 and the play-caller table
(workstream B). Near the goal line, whether a touchdown is thrown (4 points
to the quarterback) or run is a play call. The shipped QB set carries the
quarterback's expected points (which price his red-zone attempts this
season), his carries and the implied team total; nothing about how the
person calling the plays splits the red zone, and no history from before
this season.

## Hypothesis
A play-caller who throws more than expected inside the 20 turns more of his
team's red-zone trips into passing touchdowns. The habit is the person's
and follows him across teams, so his career plus this season estimates it
better than one season's handful of red-zone plays. Its effect scales with
how many red-zone trips the team gets, which the implied total predicts, so
the block also gives the tendency times the implied total. Volume (where
the touchdown chances go), not efficiency (rule #6).

## Features (`COLUMNS`)
| column | position | definition, as of the weeks before the row's week | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `pc_rz_proe` | QB | the week's play-caller (OPC) for the QB's team: `pass_oe` on his red-zone plays minus the league's red-zone `pass_oe` in the same season, summed over every earlier season as play-caller (any team) and this season with this team before the row's week (against the league over this season's earlier weeks), ÷ (his plays + 100) | nflverse play-by-play 2019–2025, the play-caller table |
| `pc_rz_proe_x_implied` | QB | `pc_rz_proe` × the team's implied points from the game's pre-game total and spread (`gridiron.vegas.implied_totals_from_nflverse`) | the above, nflverse schedules (pre-game lines) |

Red-zone play (`_tendencies.py`, `rz_proe_obs`): `play_type` pass or run
(no-plays, spikes and kneels never count), not a two-point try, not an
aborted snap, `pass_oe` present, `yardline_100` ≤ 20, 0.1 ≤ wp ≤ 0.9,
regulation, not the last two minutes of either half. A team-season has
43–156 such plays (mean 101). The league's red-zone `pass_oe` runs from
−0.5 to −5.4 points a season, so each play is measured against its own
season's league. The 100 plays of shrinkage were set before any score.

Who is in charge: as in QB-03 (the table's OPC; no row means unknown).

Missing when: the team-week has no named play-caller, or he has neither an
earlier season as play-caller nor an earlier game this season with this
team; the product also when the game has no posted total or spread.
Missing enters as 0 plus `<col>__known`.
