## Idea
Roadmap K-04, "Red-zone TD rate, offense vs defense", from play-by-play. A
red-zone trip that stalls is usually a short field goal (3 points for the
kicker); one that ends in a touchdown gives him an extra point (1). The
shipped kicker set (`baseline`, `k_ppg_season`, `k_ppg_l3`, `team_fga_pg`,
`team_xpa_pg`, `implied`, `spread`, `dome`) carries how often his team has
kicked so far and the market's expectation of its points, but nothing on
the opponent's defense beyond the line, and no split of red-zone trips into
touchdowns and stalls. QUANT_FOUNDATIONS §1.3: field goals do not scale
with the implied total; kicker points move with the line only through extra
points. The split in this matchup is what the block adds.

## Hypothesis
A kicker whose offense turns fewer red-zone trips into touchdowns than the
league, or who faces a defense that allows fewer, kicks more short field
goals than the shipped set says, and scores more. A touchdown rate is
efficiency, so it enters as a slow prior (rule #6): season to date, shrunk
hard toward the league (141 offensive and 275 defensive trips, about 2.7
and 5 seasons of a team's trips).

## Features (`COLUMNS`)
| column | position(s) | definition, as of the weeks before the row's week | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `rz_td_oe` | K | his team's red-zone trips (drives with a snap at the opponent's 20 or closer) that ended in its touchdown, minus its trips times the league's rate to date, over its trips plus 141: (TD − trips·m)/(trips + 141); its games this season before the row's week | nflverse play-by-play (`fixed_drive`, `fixed_drive_result`, `yardline_100`) |
| `opp_rz_td_allowed_oe` | K | the same for the opponent's defense: the red-zone trips it allowed that ended in a touchdown, over the league's rate, shrunk with 275 | same |

The league rate m is the touchdown share of every red-zone trip this season
before the row's week (full seasons 2019-2025: 0.560, 0.600, 0.575, 0.553,
0.540, 0.564, 0.567). Each column is the shrunk rate minus the league's, so
a league-wide shift moves no team. The prior strengths are the method of
moments (QUANT_FOUNDATIONS §5.1) on the 2012-2018 team-seasons, seasons no
fold scores, from the play-by-play alone. Drives: nflverse `fixed_drive`,
snaps of play_type pass, run, field goal, punt, kneel, spike or no-play,
extra-point and two-point tries excluded; a touchdown is
`fixed_drive_result` "Touchdown" (the offense's).

Missing when: never on these rows (from week 4 every team has played); a
team with no trip yet would be 0, the league. No `__known` indicator.

Shared code: `scripts/research/blocks/_p6_kicker_defense_pbp.py` (`drives`,
`per_team_game`, `league_as_of`, `over_league`; `python` on the file
recomputes the prior strengths). Red-zone trips per season 2019-2025:
1,697, 1,836, 1,874, 1,760, 1,766, 1,847, 1,807. On the 2,891 kicker rows
the columns' within-season SD is 0.014-0.018 (offense) and 0.008-0.010
(defense): the shrinkage leaves little, by design.
