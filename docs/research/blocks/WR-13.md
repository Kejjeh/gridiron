# WR-13: soft-tissue return ramp and the fill-in's give-back, wide receivers (pre-registered 2026-11-02)

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED.** Written before any score was seen; the only runs
before this were `--coverage-only` (coverage and the leak check, no score)
and counts of how often each column is nonzero (no actual points read).

## Idea
Roadmap WR-13, "Soft-tissue return ramp" (kind A, "shares RB-02's fit"),
with RB-02's definitions and its own block ID, cast as features. The shipped
WR set reads a returning receiver's last three games and last game
(`xfp_l3`, `snap_l3`, `tgt_share_l3`, `ay_share_l3`, `xfp_last`, `opp_last`,
`snap_last`): the games before the absence, the last one often the game he
was hurt in, with nothing saying he was away. For the receiver who filled
in, the same columns hold the weeks he started, and `vacated_pickup` drops
to zero the week the starter is back. `INJURY_EFFECTS.md` (2023-25,
descriptive, all positions) saw the six games after a hamstring absence run
at 0.84 of the earlier points per game (n = 30), and wide receivers' at
0.91 after an ankle and 0.87 after a knee absence.

## Hypothesis
A receiver's first game back from an injury absence, above all from a muscle
strain (a hamstring, a groin, a calf, the injuries that recur when a route
runner is pushed), carries fewer snaps and targets than his pre-absence
averages say; the receiver who filled in gives most of that work back the
same week. Both are volume (rule #6), read from this week's report and
roster.

## Features (`COLUMNS`)
| column | position(s) | definition, as of the weeks before the row's week | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ret_back` | WR | 1 when this is his first game back from an injury absence: he played earlier this season, his team has played at least one game since his last one (byes do not count), and between his last game and this week's report he was listed with an injury on a final report (any designation; rest days, personal matters, illness, COVID protocols and coach's decisions do not count) or was on an injury list (RES, PUP, NFI) on a weekly roster. 0 otherwise: healthy scratches, suspensions and season debuts are not returns here | box scores and snap counts ("played" = a box-score line or any snap), injury reports, weekly rosters, schedule |
| `ret_missed` | WR | the team's games he missed in that absence, capped at 8; 0 when `ret_back` is 0 | same |
| `ret_soft` | WR | `ret_back`, and the injury on his latest report entry since his last game (through this week's report) is a muscle strain: hamstring, groin, calf, quadriceps, thigh, pectoral, oblique, abdomen, adductor, hip flexor (whole words of the report text); else 0 | injury reports |
| `mate_back_opp` | WR | his share of his team's wide-receiver carries + targets in its last game × the carries + targets per game this season of the wide receivers expected back this week from injury. Expected back: a box score for the team earlier this season, no part in its last game, injured that week (as for `ret_back`), on its 53 this week (status ACT or INA, INA read as ACT) and not Out or Doubtful on this week's final report | box scores, snap counts, injury reports, weekly rosters |

Missing when: never. Every WR row gets all four columns, so none gets a
`__known` indicator.

Shared code: `scripts/research/blocks/_p2b_events_ramps_defense.py`
(`ramp`), the definitions RB-02 and TE-12 use on their positions. The helper
loads injury reports one season at a time: `Context.injuries` over
2019-2025 returns the 2025 rows alone (a runner loader bug, reported, not
changed here).

On the 13,383 WR rows of 2019-2025: `ret_back` is 1 on 613 rows (2019 63,
2020 90, 2021 124, 2022 87, 2023 73, 2024 83, 2025 93); of those, 298 missed
one game, 133 two, 72 three, 41 four and 69 five or more (19 at the cap).
`ret_soft` is 1 on 192 rows (20-37 a season), nearly a third of the returns.
`mate_back_opp` is nonzero on 2,204 rows (279-399 a season), at most 10.9.
