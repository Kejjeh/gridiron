House rules

  • Sort is VOR: points over the 12-team replacement starter (RB33 / WR41 / TE13 / QB13 under this roster). It already prices half-PPR and the two flex slots.
  • At N? is an unvalidated estimate at the target pick. Odds switches the model: ADP is the market alone; Room is a pre-draft snapshot of legal neutral-market drafts; Hybrid is exploratory. Neither uses actual opponent rosters. No blend weight is validated.
  • Tier breaks are FantasyPros consensus. Use tier breaks and roster needs together; do not reach solely because of an estimated percentage.
  • Two QBs on the clock at the same time: QB4–QB12 sit within 15 points of each other. Wait.
  • Reserve room for K and DEF and complete all starting slots.
  • Chips: usage earned 11+ expected points a game last year at a bench price; experts the multi-year-accurate rankers sit above ADP; avoid the room is ahead of both usage and the experts, or the player is unavailable; regress beat expected points by 35+ last year.
